Expand description

DMA_PAGES page-aligned pages authorized for one device, laid out per crate::ring. Never freed: the device may hold the address until reset.

Accessors take offsets from the region map crate::ring proves to lie inside the allocation, and multi-byte fields at their natural alignment; an offset past the end panics instead of touching foreign memory.
