boot: the riscv64 phases of kmain and its hooks in the neutral tail.

early runs up to and including paging; platform_init runs after the heap, up to and including the VirtIO MMIO bring-up and the deferred IOMMU. The remaining functions are called at fixed points of the neutral tail and keep the place the riscv64 code had there.

platform
riscv64 platform facts: the firmware DTB parsed against the selected SoC profile, published once into crate::platform.
