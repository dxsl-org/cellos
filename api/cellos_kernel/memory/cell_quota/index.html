Module cell_quota 

Source
Expand description

Per-cell quota accounts.

  • QUOTA_LIMITS: one atomic limit word per CellId slot, UNREGISTERED when the slot holds no incarnation; GUEST_LIMITS beside it.
  • IN_USE (memory), DMA_IN_USE and GUEST_IN_USE: one atomic counter per slot, packing the registered incarnation’s generation above its live byte count.

A charge names its owner explicitly (Charge): one Cell incarnation, or the kernel. Nothing here locks or allocates, so a charge or refund is safe inside GlobalAlloc::alloc and in interrupt context, and every refund is checked against the generation it was charged to.

GENERATION_MASK 🔒
The task generation bits a counter word keeps (the low 32). A generation is minted per incarnation (tcb::next_cell_generation; a thread takes its Cell’s), so a stale charge or refund can alias a later incarnation of its CellId only after 2^32 incarnations.
GENERATION_SHIFT 🔒
Low bits of a counter word: live bytes, at most 4 GiB - 1 per account. Every account’s bound fits: the memory and DMA limits default to DEFAULT_QUOTA_BYTES (16 MiB), the largest guest budget is 1544 MiB (catalog), and a larger registered limit is clamped to BYTES_MASK.
