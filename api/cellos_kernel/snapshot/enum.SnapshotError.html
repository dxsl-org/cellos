Expand description

Why a capture or restore was refused. Every variant is fail-closed: the caller either cold boots (restore) or leaves an image that could not possibly be replayed (capture).

Variants§

§

GateClosed

snapshot-qualified is off — the phase-01 gate.

§

HartsNotQuiesced

Not every online hart could be parked at an acknowledged safe point, so the memory image cannot be frozen. Nothing was written.

§

NoReservedScratch

No reserved capture storage: the capture cannot name its own code, stack and buffers as physical spans, so it cannot prove it is not about to save its own in-flight stack or buffer.

§

ScratchOverlapsRun

A reserved capture-scratch span intersects a planned run: the capture would save a buffer (or stack) that it is still writing.

§

NoTrustKey

No provisioned keyed-MAC key, so the header’s epoch cannot be authenticated. Refused rather than assumed.

§

NoFreshnessSource

The device has no trusted monotonic epoch source, so an image’s freshness cannot be checked. Refused rather than assumed.

§

StaleEpoch

The image’s authenticated epoch is not strictly newer than the epoch the device reports: a replay of an image this device has already consumed (or an older one).

§

AuthenticationFailed

The header’s keyed MAC does not verify: the header (epoch included) was tampered with, or was written by a different trust key.

§

UnsupportedSectorSize

Device sector size is not 512 bytes.

§

DeviceTooSmall

Device is smaller than the reserved snapshot partition.

§

CapacityExceeded

Image geometry does not fit inside the reserved partition.

§

DeviceRead

Could not read a sector.

§

DeviceWrite

Could not write a sector.

§

DeviceFlush

Could not flush pending writes.

§

NoRuns

No runs supplied / no inventory to write.

§

ImageRegionUnavailable

The mutable kernel-image span could not be derived or represented as frame runs (not delimited on this target, empty, misaligned).

§

ImageRangeOutsideImage

An image-kind run lies outside this boot’s trusted kernel-image span.

§

ImageRangeConflict

An image-kind run overlaps or duplicates an allocator-owned run.

§

BadRun

A run is empty, unaligned, out of RAM, out of order, duplicate or overlapping.

§

TooManyRuns

More runs than the format can address.

§

RunCountMismatch

Header run count does not match the inventory geometry.

§

FrameCountMismatch

Inventory frame total does not match the header frame count.

§

StateConflict

The requested transition is not allowed from the current on-disk state.

§

HeaderInvalid

Header fields (magic, version, state, kernel hash, geometry) are unusable.

§

IdentityMismatch

Header does not describe this boot (RAM layout, capacity, identity).

§

ChecksumMismatch

Canonical checksum mismatch.

§

MemoryFault

A frame could not be read from / written to physical memory.
