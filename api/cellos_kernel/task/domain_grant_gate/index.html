Publish/revoke ordering for domain_grant; host-tested on its own. Linearization of domain-grant publication against revocation.

A grant’s receiver PTE is published by GrantSlice/GrantShare under the grant-table lock, while revocation (GrantFree, unregister, task exit) deliberately runs outside that lock because it drains, shoots down and may retry. Without a shared point of order the receiver could read Live, revocation could flip to Revoking, find no receiver to drain, finish and free the frames, and the receiver would then map the freed frames into its private root.

Invariant: the Live check, the receiver mapping and the recording of the receiver all happen inside one hold of gate, and the only Live → Revoking transition happens inside a hold of the same gate. So either a publication completes (and is recorded) before revocation starts — the drain that follows removes it — or it observes Revoking and maps nothing. After revocation completes no receiver holds a mapping of the grant’s frames.

Lock order: grant table (PAGE_GRANT_TABLE/REG_GRANT_TABLE) → gate → the record’s receiver slot → the receiver root’s internal locks / KERNEL_ROOT. Revocation takes gate with no table lock held and releases it before draining; nothing here takes SCHEDULER. Every wait a gate holder performs (tag-invalidation probes) is bounded, so a revoker spinning on the gate with interrupts masked delays — never deadlocks — the publisher.
