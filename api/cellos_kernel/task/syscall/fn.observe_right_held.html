Annex S2-T/S2-W §3: GetProcs and GetProcs2 additionally need the caller’s observe_tasks right and MemInfo its observe_memory right, on top of the allowlist bit (and whatever a permit-all allowlist says). Every other opcode needs none, and neither does the kernel-context sentinel (tid 0), which has no task. A missing or terminal task is denied.
