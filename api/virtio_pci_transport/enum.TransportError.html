Expand description

Why crate::VirtioPciTransport::new (or crate::parse_caps / crate::device_type_for) refused a PCI function.

Variants§

§

InvalidVendor(u16)

The PCI vendor ID is not the virtio vendor ID 0x1af4.

§

UnknownDevice(u16)

The PCI device ID is outside the transitional and modern virtio ranges, or names a virtio device type the driver library does not know.

§

LegacyOnly

No virtio structure capability at all: the function only offers the legacy I/O-port interface, which this transport does not drive.

§

MissingCap(CfgType)

Some virtio capabilities exist but this mandatory structure is absent.

§

InvalidNotifyOffMultiplier(u32)

notify_off_multiplier is odd, so queue notify addresses would not be 16-bit aligned.

§

NotMemoryBar(u8)

A structure names a BAR that is absent, I/O, or the upper half of a 64-bit BAR.

§

BarNotAllocated(u8)

A structure names a memory BAR with no address assigned.

§

RegionOutOfBar(CfgType)

The structure’s offset + length runs past the end of its BAR.

§

RegionTooSmall(CfgType)

The structure is shorter than the registers the transport touches.

§

MapFailed(u8)

The crate::BarMapper could not map this BAR.

§

MisalignedRegion(CfgType)

The mapped structure is not aligned for its widest register access.

§

AccessPlatformMissing

crate::AccessPlatform::Required was requested but the device does not offer VIRTIO_F_ACCESS_PLATFORM.

§

ResetFailed

The device did not complete the reset new performs within crate::RESET_SPIN_BUDGET status reads, or read back as gone (0xFF). A device that cannot be quiesced is not handed to a driver.
