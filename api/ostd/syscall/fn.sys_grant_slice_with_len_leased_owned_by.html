sys_grant_slice_with_len_owned_by that also leases the Grant to the caller (GrantSlice a3 LEASE).

The kernel publishes the lease in the same grant-table transaction as the lookup and owner check. Until the caller’s next receive, its next send to owner_tid, or its death, owner_tid cannot free or unregister the Grant (PermissionDenied), and if owner_tid dies the frames stay withheld from the allocator. The returned pointer is therefore valid for reads and writes of len bytes up to that boundary, and no longer. A holder that already keeps 64 distinct leases gets None, exactly as for an unknown id.
