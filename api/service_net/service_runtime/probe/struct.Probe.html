pub(super) fn wait_net_rx(&mut self, wait_ticks: u64)

Park on NET_RX for at most wait_ticks. A recordless return means queued IPC interrupted the wait early or the finite maintenance budget elapsed. Either way, the loop retries without claiming NET_RX producer proof.
