Module relay_certificate 

Source
Expand description

Strict DER framing and identity binding for the mounted client chain.

Bounds are frozen: at most RELAY_CHAIN_MAX_CERTS certificates and RELAY_CHAIN_MAX_LEN bytes. The leaf must carry the clientAuth EKU and must not carry the serverAuth EKU; the served SPKI from KMS (opcode 14) must hash to the deployment NodeId.
