VMware PVSCSI Driver Cell — Tier-1 Privileged Driver Cell (S3 phase 09).

PVSCSI (15AD:07C0) is the paravirtual SCSI host VMware recommends; QEMU models it as -device pvscsi. The cell claims the first adapter by exact ID (424), takes its 32 KiB register window from the 434 function record, resets it, hands it one-page request and completion rings in one GrantDma-authorized allocation (SETUP_RINGS), finds the first direct-access disk through scsi-block, publishes the block route of its BDF (BlockDevice::Pci, 416) and serves the DrvRequest sector-I/O protocol to the registered VFS only — the virtio-scsi pattern.

Completions are interrupt-driven (H-S3-5): 435 grants MSI-X or MSI and every wait parks on WaitCompletion(IRQ) inside the command’s time budget; without a vector the cell polls and says why (irq=polled reason=…).

Law 4 exception: unsafe is confined to the DMA area (dma.rs); MMIO goes through the bounds-checked ostd::mmio::MmioRegion.
