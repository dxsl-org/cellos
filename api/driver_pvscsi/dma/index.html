Module dma 

Source
Expand description

The adapter’s one DMA allocation: ring state, request ring, completion ring, sense buffer and data bounce buffer, laid out by crate::rings.

Law 4 exception: the volatile accesses below touch device-visible memory. The allocation is owned for the cell’s lifetime (the device may hold its addresses until reset), it is authorized for the adapter’s BDF before any of its addresses is programmed, and every access is bounds-checked against its length; 32-bit fields are naturally aligned by the layout.
