Module irq 

Source
Expand description

Completion waits: MSI-X/MSI wake-ups (H-S3-5) inside a time budget.

An interrupt is only a hint to re-read the completion ring: every park is bounded by PARK_TICKS, every return re-reads the ring, and the whole wait is bounded by the command’s budget on the monotonic clock, so a lost, masked or revoked interrupt degrades to slow polling, never to a hang. Before each park the completion causes are acknowledged (INTR_STATUS is write-1-to-clear) and the ring re-read, so the next completion raises a fresh message. A refusal by 435 or 242 keeps the polled path and says why (irq=polled reason=…).

Each park that ends by its deadline is classified by the ring re-read that follows it (phase-09 D6): ring=ready means the completion was already there but no message woke the cell (a late or lost interrupt); ring=empty means the device had not completed yet (a slow host). Both counts and the time since the wait began are logged.
