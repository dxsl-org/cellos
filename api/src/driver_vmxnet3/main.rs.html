1//! VMware vmxnet3 NIC Driver Cell — Tier-1 Privileged Driver Cell (S3 phase 09).
2//!
3//! vmxnet3 (`15AD:07B0`) is the paravirtual NIC VMware recommends; QEMU models
4//! it as `-device vmxnet3`. Through the shared `nic_driver` skeleton the cell:
5//!   1. Names its controller exactly with `sys_find_pcie_device_by_vendor`
6//!      (424) over `identity::QUERY_DEVICE_IDS` and repeats the identity check
7//!      (`identity::classify`) before touching MMIO.
8//!   2. Reads BAR0 (PT: doorbells, vector masks) and BAR1 (VD: commands) from
9//!      the 434 function record and claims both.
10//!   3. Negotiates revision 1, resets the device, reads the permanent MAC,
11//!      allocates MSI-X through 435 (H-S3-4 b: bounded bring-up, link and
12//!      TX-completion waits only), places `DriverShared`, the queue
13//!      descriptors, one TX and one RX queue and every frame buffer in one
14//!      `GrantDma`-authorized allocation, and activates the device.
15//!   4. Registers as the NIC driver only after bring-up and serves the
16//!      unchanged Tx/Rx/GetMac wire. RX stays request-driven: the net service
17//!      asks for frames, so the RX vector stays masked (`rx=request-driven`).
18//!
19//! Law 4 exception: `unsafe` is confined to the DMA area (`dma.rs`); MMIO goes
20//! through the bounds-checked `ostd::mmio::MmioRegion`.
21//!
22//! `PcieDriverCap` is granted by init via the reviewed `/bin/vmxnet3` launch
23//! edge, not a manifest flag.
24
