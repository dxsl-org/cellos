BLOCK_DRIVER_WAIT_MS 🔒
How long init waits for a block driver to register (Phase 10 S10-3c, D3c-2 B): one NVMe admin budget. Before, the wait was 400 yields, after which the VFS mounted without a disk even while the NVMe Cell was still identifying its controller.
BLOCK_DRIVER_WAIT_YIELDS 🔒
The former window: used for the other drivers once the NVMe Cell is gone (no controller, or not in the image), and as the whole bound without a monotonic clock.

block_wait_step 🔒
One step of the block-driver wait: done once a driver registered or the time budget is spent; wait on while the NVMe Cell lives; otherwise (it exited, it was never started, or there is no clock) the former window. The AHCI Cell idles without exiting on a machine without a controller, so only the NVMe Cell’s life extends the wait.
