Cell Frame geometry, hit testing, and rasterization (CellOS Linear).

A Cell Tab (ADR-0027) sits on the top-left of the Cell Frame and holds only the app icon and the title, as in Haiku. The icon opens the window menu (maximize/restore, minimize, close); a double click on the title toggles maximize. Everything the compositor draws is 1px hairlines on the pixel grid with lightly rounded outer corners; a soft drop shadow lives in the invisible SHADOW_MARGIN, and the innermost GRIP pixels of it resize.

Vertical layout above the content: TITLE rows of tab, the last of which is the hairline shared by the tab and the frame body.
