Cellos SSH client core.

This crate wraps the vendored Sunset (third_party/sunset, see its PROVENANCE and PATCHES). Two Cellos patches apply there:

  • KEX is curve25519-sha256 only, the cipher is chacha20-poly1305@openssh.com only, and strict KEX is required (command-contracts §4.2);
  • entropy comes only from the source installed with install_entropy. With no source installed, or after a failed draw, KEX does not start.
