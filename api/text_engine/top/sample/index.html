Turn validated telemetry snapshots into per-frame samples.

The Sampler keeps one baseline per row, (generation, cell_id, cpu_ns) for Cells and ((generation, tid), cpu_ns) for tasks, in the provider’s order, and matches the next snapshot against it with one linear merge-join. No snapshot bytes and no history are kept.
