Crate recovery_worker 

Source
Expand description

recovery-worker: serves the demo counter but owns no business state. It attaches to its authority with the one-shot nonce its parent staged, refuses to serve unless the restored schema is its own, and commits every mutation at the authority before replying (CP1 §4.2). Registration (Ready) is the authority’s decision, never the worker’s.

Modules§

common 🔒
IPC and timing helpers shared by the three recovery-demo Cells; each Cell uses a subset.
serve 🔒
The worker’s request loop. Every mutation is looked up and then committed at the authority under the worker’s epoch before the client sees a reply, so a crash at any point leaves either no effect or a committed, reconcilable one. Each client request is answered exactly once, through its own [Incoming].
