What a pass proves: the kernel wrote the initial stack into the root user stack
and entered with sp at argc; ostd's `_start` passed it to mlibc's strong
`__cellos_libc_start`; mlibc built its Tcb and static TLS block from the
program headers it received through AT_PHDR, ran the constructors, and ran
thread, futex, timer and ELF-introspection paths on x86_64 inside a private root.

Not proven here: aarch64 (no compiler on the host), RV64 mlibc runtime (the
disk-image path did not resolve /bin/mlibc-smoke; RV64 boot with the new
initial stack was checked separately), `-cpu host` under KVM (the guest exits
during boot selftests, before any Cell; its last line is PKU-SELFTEST, which
precedes X86-VMM-SMOKE on a VMX host [INFERENCE: the VMM smoke is the cause]),
physical hardware.

Repeated runs (one boot, TCG): `MLIBC_POSIX_RUNS=100` passed 44 consecutive
posix runs; the 45th spawn was refused before the Cell started
("[fs] OOM: VIFS1 read of 792712 bytes", "PrepareChild refused … OutOfMemory").
`free` around 10 runs: +564 KiB used after `mlibc-posix-x86` (8 threads per run),
+0 after 10 × `mlibc-smoke` and 10 × `ps` (no threads). The growth follows
thread exits in a Tier-2 Cell [INFERENCE: kernel thread lifecycle; the witness
threads use only Spawn, Exit and futexes]. Phase R3's 100-run criterion stays open.

Slice TL (2026-11-04) — leak fixed. Cause: the scheduler's switch plan kept
the incoming domain referenced across an exiting task's last switch, so a
threaded Cell's domain was never released (no DOMAIN-FRAME-RELEASE after any
posix run; root AddressSpace strong count 6 instead of 1-2 at retirement).
