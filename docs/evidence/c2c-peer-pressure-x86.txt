C2C Phase 03: finite competing producers under peer-mailbox pressure (2026-10-09)

Scope: new fixture-only witness in cells/tests/bench/src/scenarios/local_service_lifecycle.rs and bench-probe role dispatch. No production kernel/SDK or ABI changes. Live kernel peer mailbox depth is 64 (kernel/src/task/tcb.rs: HOTSWAP_MSG_QUEUE_DEPTH = 64).

Scenario and observed behavior:
- Coordinator A fills the provider's 64-slot mailbox while provider holds for 100 ticks in sys_set_timer(100).
- Independent caller B (spawned without prior operations) attempts submit; provider's mailbox is full, so submit returns IpcSubmitError::Busy before provider timer expires (within 50 ticks setup margin).
- When provider wakes, both caller A (128 total calls) and caller B (64 total calls) pump calls and take completions concurrently.
- pump_pressure retries only on Busy refusals before admission; never resends accepted work. Each taken completion validates 3-byte payload, exact producer and sequence, and checks that retired token try_take returns InvalidOperation.
- Provider processes 192 total calls (128 from A, 64 from B), validates sender identity, rejects duplicates, sends exact 3-byte replies, and verifies mailbox_empty() at the end.
- Observed markers:

Evidence ceiling:
This demonstrates finite contention progress and Busy backpressure under peer mailbox saturation in software-only x86 QEMU. It does NOT prove starvation freedom or bounded completion latency under hostile, infinite, or weighted queue monopolization. Multi-source completion waiting, deterministic two-hart publication/wake, abandoned-caller grant lifetime, physical Intel qualification and remote RPC remain separate open obligations.
