Notes
-----
  * The bounded-queue refusal is the legacy oracle's own `busy_frame=0x7f01` on
    18 attempts with `queue_peak=16`, i.e. congestion is a refusal, not a drop
    (`indeterminate=0`, `stale=0`, `correlation=0`, `silent_drop=0`).
  * `restart status=PASS ... stale_send=INDETERMINATE` is the local half of the
    stale-binding contract: after the broker respawns with a new tid, a send to the
    old one is reported indeterminate, not silently retried.
  * `idle_ipc_wake` is honest about its own ceiling: one recordless return landed
    under `proof_ceiling_ticks`, one did not, so the verdict is
    `sub-ceiling-wake` on this host. A previous run of the same lane reported
    `quantum-spanning-idle-out` for the same sub-metric, which is why the lane
    asserts the drain/return bounds rather than a fixed verdict.
  * No remote route is involved: this is the local broker oracle only.

Before/after for Phase-02's second success criterion (2026-10-09)

That criterion asks for "measured source-bound before/after p99 and no watchdog misses; no
unmeasured improvement claim", so this lane was re-run twice on the tree that now carries Phase-02
slices A and B and the Phase-03 opt-in API, and compared with the run above (made before slices A
and B landed). Two "after" runs are recorded to bound this lane's own run-to-run spread rather than
to pick a flattering one.

  after 1 : c2c-broker-oracle-qemu-local-after-1.log

Source-bound, not raw-wall-clock: the calibrated direct-IPC reference the lane measures for itself
is `direct_ipc_ref_ns=147000` in **all three** runs with `calibration=MEASURED`, so the comparison is
against the same host, clock and build provenance. p99 is expressed as a multiple of that reference:

Reading it: at every concurrency above one, both after runs are at or below the before run, and the
two after runs' own spread at n=8 is ~7% — the difference is inside the lane's spread, which is why
this is reported as **no regression**, not as an improvement. n=1 shows the largest relative spread
(2.66–3.49) and is the single-sample case, i.e. the noisiest reading in the table.
