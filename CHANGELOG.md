## [Unreleased]

### 🚀 Improvements
- Direction: Intel x86-64 Cell-to-Cell Anywhere is the sole active program (ADR-0022, 2026-10-08). Every task must serve a named C2C deliverable/dependency or required baseline repair; other hardware/product/runtime programs are parked without deleting code or evidence. Target one fixed headless Intel configuration, then a separately qualified second Intel node (matching configuration is preferred, not required); Tier 2/C++ and Intel VMX gaps remain explicit. No purchase, ABI/security exception, remote activation or production qualification is implied.
- C2C Law-1 checkpoint 2 recorded — `LookupServiceBound = 429` is **FROZEN**: the accountable maintainer gave the second, separate confirmation after reviewing the implemented ABI delta and evidence, so `libs/api`'s new surface can no longer be removed, renamed, re-laid-out or extended without the ABI process again (including two fresh confirmations). Two implementation-level deviations from the checkpoint-1 design were presented and accepted with it: a short buffer returns `SyscallError::BufferTooSmall` (matching the existing fixed-record writers `QueryDirHandles` and `ResolveCellOwner`) rather than `InvalidInput`; and a provider whose recorded identity is not live answers `0` (no binding the kernel cannot stand behind) while `LookupService`'s tid answer is unaffected. The record pins the confirmed revision's digests and a new check keeps it honest: `scripts/check-lookupservicebound-law1-digests.sh` asserts every item expressible as code text — the opcode and its decode arm, the shared allowlist bit 37, the 24-byte record length and all three fields, `is_live`'s three-nonzero rule, `from_bytes`' rejection of a non-live record, the deliberate absence of a reserved field, the module registration, the client wrapper, and the kernel variant/allowlist/decode/dispatch arms — failing with a per-item message. Verified both ways: it passes on the frozen tree and fails on a one-value mutation (`SERVICE_BINDING_LEN` 24 → 25, restored afterwards). Record: [`.agents/260927-1100-c2c-anywhere-tier-aware/law1-lookupservicebound.md`](.agents/260927-1100-c2c-anywhere-tier-aware/law1-lookupservicebound.md) (`CONTRIBUTING.md:70-71`, `docs/code-standards.md:40-45`, ADR-0013 decision #8).
- C2C local binding implemented (`LookupServiceBound = 429`): under Law-1 checkpoint 1 and the granted kernel-repair file-owner handoff, ADR-0023's single additive opcode landed. `libs/api` gains `ViSyscall::LookupServiceBound = 429` and `abi::service_binding::{SERVICE_BINDING_LEN, ServiceBinding}` — a fixed 24-byte `#[repr(C)] { tid, cell_id, generation }` record with little-endian `to_bytes`/`from_bytes` and an `is_live()` requiring all three nonzero; a `From<usize>` decode arm; and an allowlist arm that **shares bit 37** with `LookupService` (the `u64` allowlist is full — bits 0–62 syscalls, 63 the VFS-mutate declaration — so a fresh bit was never available and no cell's pre-existing `__ViCell_syscalls` section is silently denied). `LookupService = 206` keeps byte-exact behaviour, no send opcode is added, and no existing cell needs re-signing. ABI: `a0 = service_id`, `a1 = out_ptr`, `a2 = out_len` → 24 bytes written; `0` for no live provider (also for a paused provider, preserving the hot-swap quiesce barrier, and for a provider whose recorded identity is not live, so the kernel never states a binding it cannot stand behind); `SyscallError::BufferTooSmall` for a short buffer, never a partial write. The kernel service registry now records and returns the provider's `(tid, cell_id, generation)`, captured from live scheduler state at every registration site (supervisor, driver self-registration, hot-swap cutover), and `ostd` gains the `sys_lookup_service_bound` wrapper. `LocalEndpoint::call` is deliberately unchanged — moving the SDK onto the bounded exact-operation primitive is C2C Phase 03 work. Verified: `cargo test -p api` 109 passed (incl. the record round-trip/zero-field/short-slice tests and an allowlist-sharing test pinning 429), `cargo test -p ostd` 43+7+19 passed, 123 broker tests passed, kernels build for riscv64/aarch64/x86_64, and one x86_64 QEMU production boot reports `VFS-BINDING tid=4 cell=1 gen=109 matches_lookup=true` plus `ABSENT-BINDING lookup=None bound_is_none=true` while the lifecycle legs still show the synchronous caller stranded and the bounded call terminal — `docs/evidence/local-service-lifecycle-x86-qemu.{txt,log}`. The surface was **FROZEN** later the same day at Law-1 checkpoint 2 (see the entry above), after the two accepted deviations.
- Kernel invariant guard (ADR-0023 §2.5): task ids are never re-issued within one boot, and that property is load-bearing for local IPC safety (a cached endpoint naming a dead tid must fail closed), the service registry (a tid names exactly one provider for the boot) and the exact-operation peer binding. The two bare `next_task_id += 1` sites now use a `checked_add`-based `Scheduler::advance_task_id` that fails closed on exhaustion instead of wrapping to `0` — the reserved "none"/"no provider" sentinel — matching the cell/ELF path's existing form. A boot-time `test-hooks` guard, `kernel::task::task_id_selftest`, spawns real threads through the ordinary syscall path, retires each through the real death funnel, and asserts no number is re-issued and the sequence strictly increases; it snapshots and restores `next_task_id`, removes its synthetic parent and reaps every thread, so the boot sequence is undisturbed. Witnessed on the AArch64 `test-hooks` lane: `[selftest] TASK-ID-REUSE: PASS (monotonic across spawn/exit)` with the guest suite continuing to 96 PASS / 0 FAIL (`docs/evidence/task-id-reuse-guard-aarch64-qemu.{txt,log}`).

Fixed with the file's own idiom, `#[cfg_attr(not(target_arch = "x86_64"), allow(dead_code))]`, matching its neighbour `dma_without_remapper`; no behaviour change.

Narrowed to a **frame-accounting** delta across `prepare_elf_task` + drop — only `free-frames` genuinely differs, and the `field=state` line is that reporter's generic banner (`report_task_mismatches` prints nothing, so every task's state is identical). - C2C local lifecycle witness (x86_64 QEMU): a dead provider now has a measured outcome on the Intel target. `bench local-service-lifecycle` (`cells/tests/bench/src/scenarios/local_service_lifecycle.rs`) drives both shipped IPC surfaces against the same provider task, which consumes one request and then calls `sys_exit(0)` without replying — the ordinary `exit_task` path. **Synchronous leg** (`ostd::ipc::service_call_typed`, the path `LocalEndpoint::call` / `ServiceRef::call` use): the caller's handoff completes (proved by the provider's own `provider-exiting-without-reply` line, which it can only print after `sys_recv` delivered the request), the caller then parks in a sender-masked `Recv`, and it is **never woken** — `SYNC-RESULT=RETURNED` never appears and the caller is reclaimed only by the orchestrator's `sys_force_exit`, which itself proves it was still live and blocked. This is the gap the kernel documents at `kernel/src/task/scheduler.rs:1279-1284`. **Asynchronous leg** (bounded exact-operation `submit`/`wait`/`take`, peer bound as `(cell_id, cell_generation)`, `async_ipc::peer_died` terminal at `kernel/src/task/async_ipc.rs:298-307`, wired at `scheduler.rs:1324-1325`): same dead peer, same kernel, `ASYNC-TERMINAL=PEER-GONE`. Built by `scripts/build-x86_64-c2c-lifecycle-ci.sh` — a new isolated x86 lane (production feature set, no `test-hooks`; own `CARGO_TARGET_DIR`, `EMBEDDED_OVERRIDE` and ISO root) that packages `/bin/bench` + `/bin/bench-probe`; no tracked bash x86 image script did that before. Driven by `tests/integration/tests/local-service-lifecycle-x86.rs`, which also **fails** against an image without `/bin/bench`, so its assertions cannot pass vacuously. Raw log `docs/evidence/local-service-lifecycle-x86-qemu.log`, summary `docs/evidence/local-service-lifecycle-x86-qemu.txt`. Witness only: no kernel, ABI, Spec-17 or production call-site change.
- Build/CI repair (blocking, pre-existing): `scripts/unsafe-allowlist.toml` allowlisted `cells/services/net/src/tls_handler.rs`, which no longer contains `unsafe`, and omitted `cells/services/net/src/tls/dispatch.rs`, where the raw-pointer plumbing actually moved (`poll_future` erases the `SocketSet<'_>` borrow into the task-local TLS context). `cellos-sign`'s F1 gate consequently refused **every** image signing step in the tree (`FAIL: F1 — Cells must be #![forbid(unsafe_code)]`), which blocked the x86, AArch64 and RV64 lanes alike. - C2C Law-1 checkpoint 1 recorded: the accountable maintainer approved the **design** of the single additive opcode from [ADR-0023](docs/decisions/0023-local-service-generation-binding.md) §2.3. Record with the exact 14-item list, the compatibility-consequence review and the bound digests: [`.agents/260927-1100-c2c-anywhere-tier-aware/law1-lookupservicebound.md`](.agents/260927-1100-c2c-anywhere-tier-aware/law1-lookupservicebound.md). The confirmed shape is `LookupServiceBound = 429` with `a0 = service_id`, `a1 = out_ptr`, `a2 = out_len`, returning `SERVICE_BINDING_LEN` (24) bytes written on success, `0` for no live provider (shared with `LookupService`'s absence sentinel, and also returned for a `Paused` provider so the hot-swap quiesce barrier holds), and `SyscallError::InvalidInput` for a short buffer — never a partial write. The record is `libs/api/src/abi/service_binding.rs`: `#[repr(C)] ServiceBinding { tid: u64, cell_id: u64, generation: u64 }` with little-endian `to_bytes`/`from_bytes` and an `is_live()` requiring all three nonzero, deliberately **without** a reserved field (a future need is a new opcode, which is this repository's append-only precedent). `LookupService = 206` keeps its byte-exact behaviour, no send opcode is added, the addition shares allowlist bit 37 rather than consuming a fresh bit (the `u64` allowlist is full: bits 0–62 syscalls, 63 VFS-mutate), and **no existing cell needs re-signing**. This is checkpoint 1 only: it permits editing `libs/api/` for these items, freezes nothing, and is explicitly **not** the kernel-repair file-owner handoff that the registry record and the two `next_task_id` guard sites still require; checkpoint 2 must review the implemented ABI delta with its bound verification evidence as a separate owner decision (`CONTRIBUTING.md:70-71`, `docs/code-standards.md:40-45`).
- C2C local binding decision: [ADR-0023](docs/decisions/0023-local-service-generation-binding.md) sets the local binding axis. Source review corrected a premise: within one boot a task id is never re-issued (`Scheduler.next_task_id` is a single monotonic counter, `kernel/src/task/scheduler.rs:290,333`, `kernel/src/task/launch.rs:118,246,251`; no free list exists), so a stale cached `LocalEndpoint` TID fails closed through `TargetGone` (`kernel/src/task.rs:2166`) instead of misdelivering; the reuse that is real is on `CellId` slots, which is why a per-Cell `cell_generation` epoch exists (`kernel/src/task/tcb.rs:547-556,479-493`). The decision therefore binds on the existing `(cell_id, generation)` axis, records the provider binding in the kernel service registry (kernel-internal), proposes exactly **one** additive opcode `LookupServiceBound = 429` returning `{tid, cell_id, generation}` while `LookupService = 206` stays byte-compatible, adds **no** send opcode, and moves local service calls onto the already-shipped bounded exact-operation IPC path — closing the kernel's own documented gap that a reply waiter which already left `Sending` is not woken on provider death. Also recorded: the TID non-reuse invariant is load-bearing and unguarded, and the two bare `next_task_id += 1` sites would wrap to `0` in release builds. **No kernel, ABI, Spec-17 or remote change was made** by the decision itself: the registry record and both guard sites still need the kernel-repair owner handoff, and opcode 429 needed Law-1 approval — checkpoint 1 was recorded on 2026-10-08 and checkpoint 2 is outstanding (`CONTRIBUTING.md:70-71`, `docs/code-standards.md:40-45`). Spec 20 draft amended to v3.1 to point at the decision; it remains unratified.

- cells/driver-dwc2-usb: **self-test loopback PHY cho LAN9514, bật bằng `CELLOS_DEBUG_LOOPBACK=1` khi dựng ảnh.** Board netboot qua chính sợi dây đó — nên dây, PHY và chip đã được chứng minh dưới U-Boot — nhưng dưới Cellos **không một frame nào vào tới net service**: `[net-bridge] first e1000 RX` và mốc mới `first frame while guest registered` đều không in trong cả lượt, kể cả khi PC đang ARP, còn `ping 192.168.42.50` từ PC trả `Destination host unreachable` (không có ARP reply). Câu hỏi còn lại nằm bên trong chip: đường nhận có chạy không. Với `BMCR.LOOPBACK` (`bit 14`), PHY đưa frame vừa phát trở lại đường nhận, nên **một frame quay về** chứng minh MAC TX + MAC RX + RX FIFO + bulk-IN đều chạy và lỗi nằm ở dây/đầu bên kia; **không quay về** thì lỗi ở cấu hình nhận hoặc chính transfer nhận. Test chạy một lần lúc bring-up, in `[lan9514] loopback diag: chip_tx_ok=… chip_rx_bytes=…`, và **phục hồi PHY** trước khi trả về nên ảnh chẩn đoán vẫn phục vụ traffic bình thường. Công tắc tách hẳn khỏi các trace (nó *đổi trạng thái chip* chứ không chỉ in thêm), và cả bốn cấu hình feature build 0 warning. **Board trả lời ngay lượt đầu: `[lan9514] loopback diag: chip_tx_ok=true chip_rx_bytes=64`** ⇒ MAC TX + PHY + MAC RX + RX FIFO + **bulk-IN của driver** đều chạy, nên giả thuyết "chip không nhận" bị loại. Cùng ảnh đó nay in thêm **snapshot chỉ-đọc trước mọi ghi của driver** (`pre-init: HW_CFG/MAC_CR/PM_CTL/ADDRL/ADDRH` + `pre-init phy: BMCR/BMSR/ANAR/ANLPAR/PHYSCS`) — vì board netboot qua chính dây đó nên trạng thái *U-Boot để lại* là mốc so sánh, và cái driver ghi đè lên nó là nghi phạm còn lại duy nhất.
- cells/service-net: **một mốc một-lần cho frame vào *không* dành cho guest.** Sau khi hạn-thời-gian-bus làm TX của guest đi trọn (`[net-bridge] first guest TX accepted=true` + `[hv-virtio-host] net-tx-complete`), thứ còn thiếu chỉ là `first guest RX len=…` — mà từ log thì "chip không đưa gì vào" và "LAN đang nói chuyện, chỉ là không nói với guest" **nhìn giống hệt nhau**. `route_rx` nay in **một lần** khi có frame tới lúc guest đã đăng ký MAC nhưng không gửi cho guest, kèm **cả hai địa chỉ** (`dst=… guest=… len=…`): chính cặp địa chỉ đó tách hai khả năng và quyết định bước tiếp theo nằm ở đường nhận của chip hay ở đầu dây bên kia. Không thêm dòng nào khi guest chưa đăng ký hoặc khi frame tới đúng guest (nhánh đó đã có `first guest RX`).

- tests/native-domain: **a real two-Cell RV64 Tier-2 grant pair is driven through the public syscall path.** `scripts/qemu-native-domain-test.sh --case grant-pair` boots a fresh throwaway image whose VIFS1 store carries `cells/tests/tier2-grant-owner` and `tier2-grant-receiver` (both real `TaskAddressSpace::Domain` tasks, admitted by the reviewed Tier-2 launch edges) and drives the owner through `GrantAlloc`/`GrantRegister`/`GrantSlice`/`GrantShare`/`GrantFree`/`GrantUnregister`, handing the observed sentinel to a second, distinct domain. Every phase-01 denial is asserted verbatim (`OWNER-ALLOC: DENY`, `OWNER-SLICE0: DENY`, `OWNER-SHARE-RO: DENY target=0`, `RECEIVER-SLICE-RO: DENY id=0`, `RECEIVER-FRAME-REUSE: REFUSED`, …, `h1-grant-pair-WpVt7F`); the 1-hart regression `admission`, `asid-lease`, `unmap-order`, `grant-revoke`, `grant-gate` all PASS. The positive branches are unreachable while containment denies allocation, so no grant lifecycle behaviour is enabled and no production or qualification claim follows from this lane.
- tests/native-domain: **the Tier-2 grant pair now asserts the lifecycle's real behaviour, the same-recipient downgrade has its own witness, and the deferred-ack outcome is asserted in invariant form rather than relabelled.** This supersedes the "positive branches are unreachable" caveat of the entry above: the runner asserts the *positive* contract (`OWNER-MAPPED: OK`, `OWNER-REG-MAPPED: OK`, `RECEIVER-RW: OK`, `RECEIVER-SLICE-RO: OK`, `OWNER-FREE: OK`, `OWNER-UNREGISTER: OK`, `OWNER-EXIT: OK`, `OWNER: PASS`, `RECEIVER-FRAME-REUSE: REFUSED`) and classifies every deliberate store fault to the exact grant address the owner handed over.
