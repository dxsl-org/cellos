## [Unreleased]

### 🚀 Improvements
- kernel/argv + ostd/shell: **command line của cell không còn bị mất.** Hai lỗi độc lập trên cùng một đường: (1) `StagedSpawnArgvCleanup` được dựng bằng `bool::then_some(...)` — `then_some` nhận tham số *theo giá trị*, nên guard tồn tại ở **mọi** syscall và bị drop ngay khi predicate sai, và `Drop` của nó xoá argv vừa stage ⇒ bất kỳ syscall nào chen giữa `set_spawn_argv` và spawn (chính là VFS-read bên trong `sys_spawn_from_path`) đều xoá sạch: `httpd 9091 /tmp/resp.txt` nghe ở `:8080`, `tier2-exploit peer` chạy mode NULL, `bench peer-death-guard` chạy suite mặc định; (2) attempt bị **từ chối** cũng xoá argv theo thiết kế, nên khi shell fallback từ route Elf (bị từ chối với target mang quyền như `bench`) sang route Path thì command line đã mất — shell nay thử `sys_spawn_from_path_raw` trước và **publish lại** argv trước khi fallback (chỉ caller làm được: `set_spawn_argv` là slot của chính nó, `sys_spawn_args` là one-shot). Kèm theo `cells/tests/posix-shim-test` bổ sung `GetRandom` vào allowlist (cell lắp cho fstat smoke, các smoke entropy/socket thêm sau bị deny). Kiểm chứng local: `hotswap-smoke` 15/15, `tier2-fault-isolation` 5/5, boot `network_*` 7/7 (một case wget flaky), `mqtt_*` 2/2, `posix_shim_*` 3/3.
- ostd/actor: **ứng dụng tự khai báo supervision tree, không cần sửa `/bin/init`.** `ostd::actor` là vòng mailbox actor trên envelope `0xAC` sẵn có — dispatch postcard có kiểu, `call`/`reply` recv **masked theo peer tid** (Spec 17 §2), lifecycle `on_start`/`on_tick`/`on_shutdown`, và log to cho message không decode được (§7, không silent drop). `ostd::actor::supervisor` thêm bảng con **động** với `Policy` (Permanent/Transient/Temporary), intensity theo cửa sổ (mặc định ≤5 restart/~10 s, kiểm tra *trước* khi tăng — đúng thứ tự của `init`), **give-up chỉ với đứa con đó**, backoff mũ có trần, và `Strategy` one_for_one/one_for_all/rest_for_one. Không đổi `libs/api`, không thêm syscall/opcode/discriminant: message actor nằm trong envelope `0xAC 0x00` nên `Shutdown`/`CapRevoked`/hotswap vẫn về cùng mailbox. ADR-0021 chốt thiết kế (monitor vẫn gate `SpawnCap`, namespace vẫn service id `u16`). 8 host test cho quyết định thuần (policy/intensity/backoff/strategy); `ostd` nay bật được harness host theo tiền lệ `libs/api` (`#![cfg_attr(not(test), no_std)]`).
- cells/backend + kernel/launch_profile: **witness B0 chạy thật trên RV64 QEMU** (`scripts/qemu-actor-supervisor.sh`): một supervisor spawn 3 worker, ping bằng typed call, tự `ForceExit` một worker → restart trong **≤100 tick (<1 s)** kèm exit reason quan sát được, rồi 6 exit bất thường trong một cửa sổ → give-up đúng đứa đó trong khi 2 worker còn lại vẫn trả lời, và một tree `one_for_all` 2 con chứng minh cả kẻ lỗi lẫn anh em được dựng lại. Launch edge là một dòng exact `backend-supervisor → /bin/backend-worker` (`Path|Elf`, ceiling rỗng, `requires_lifecycle_authority=true`); `/bin/backend-supervisor` phải nằm trong VIFS1 vì kernel từ chối ceiling khác rỗng trên route `SpawnFromElf` (bytes do caller cấp) — cùng chỗ `hotswap-demo` đang nằm.
- kernel/revocation: **runtime capability revocation is real now, not a label change.** `sys_cap_revoke` used to clear TCB fields and nothing else — every authority already handed out stayed live, which is exactly the stale-authority retention `spec/16` cites the J-Kernel proof for (LBI prevents forgery, not revocation). Revoking an `mmio_devices` bit now releases the window in the resource registry and takes its *user* accessibility away (`paging::revoke_mmio_user`: x86 clears the user PTE, riscv64/aarch64 lower the permission with `protect_page` so the frame stays identity-mapped for the kernel), reclaims the target's owned grants (a revoked device class can be the authority behind a grant, and grants carry no source-cap tag), and then clears the field — teardown first, so a teardown that cannot finish returns `NotSupported` with the capability intact. Verified on RV64 QEMU: `MMIO-REVOKE-CLASS`, `MMIO-REVOKE-ALLOWLIST`, `MMIO-REVOKE-USERBIT`.
- kernel/iommu: `iommu::unmap_dma` is no longer a no-op stub. `Sv39IommuPt`/`VtdSlpt` gained a lookup-only `unmap_range` (missing child tables are skipped, never allocated; intermediate tables are never freed, so a sibling range keeps its mapping), the x86 leg zeroes the SLPT leaves and drains the domain's IOTLB, and the riscv leg acknowledges the batch with one IOTINVAL.VMA + IOFENCE. `iommu::cleanup_cell` became `iommu::revoke_dma_for_cell`: one teardown, reached from cell death *and* from a runtime revoke, so a revoked driver Cell ends in the hardware state it would reach by dying. Verified: `IOMMU-TEARDOWN-LEAF/IDEMPOTENT/LOOKUP-ONLY/REMAPPABLE`.
- kernel/grants: a Cell's owned grants can be reclaimed without waiting for it to die — `reclaim_owned_grants(tid)` is the reaper's owner-side pass, extracted so death and revoke share one implementation, and it routes every frame through the same pin transaction as death: a region an in-flight operation still holds is **quarantined, not freed** (D36), and returned when the driver acknowledges. Grants the target merely *received* are untouched — they belong to another owner's authority. Verified: `GRANT-RECLAIM-OWNED/RECEIVED/PINNED`.
- kernel/revocation: `pcie_driver`, `platform` and `supervisor` are revocable end to end. Three additive `cap_mask` bits (`PCIE_DRIVER = 1<<4`, `PLATFORM = 1<<5`, `SUPERVISOR = 1<<6`; Law-1 confirmed twice) dispatch their eager teardown: `pcie_driver` drains the whole DMA domain plus its requester entries, releases its BDFs and its BAR windows and reclaims its grants; `platform` releases the ECAM config-space window; `supervisor` is a field clear (kernel-minted, never propagated, no ambient resource behind it). `task::teardown_dma_authority` is shared with the retirement path. Verified: `REVOKE-PRIVILEGED` inside `thread-cap self-test PASS`.
- kernel/ipc + ostd: a victim is told what it lost. The kernel sends `[0xAC, 0xF2, mask_le4]` fire-and-forget after the teardown, `spec/17` §3 registers `0xF2` as `CapRevoked` under the `0xAC` namespace, and `ostd` decodes it into `AppEvent::CapRevoked { mask }` (additive; cells already carry a wildcard arm). Delivery is best-effort and never fails the revoke — the teardown is what enforces it. Host tests pin the wire contract and the truncated-envelope case.
- port-platform/kernel: a narrow C child-launch adapter (`libs/port-platform/{include/,}cellos_spawn.{h,c}`) publishes `cellos_spawn`, `cellos_child_wait`, `cellos_spawn_argv_raw`, `cellos_spawn_argv_split`, và `cellos_spawn_receive_grants`, ghép đúng một reviewed launch edge (`(caller, route, target)`, ceil CapSet rỗng) với command line đã stage và các grant endpoint tường minh qua `PipeShare` — handle không bao giờ đi trong argv. Witness Tier-2 `cells/tests/c-spawn` + `c-spawn-child` (`scripts/qemu-c-spawn.sh`) chứng minh trên RV64 QEMU harts 1 và 2: admission Tier 2 cho cả hai cell, argv 2 item tới byte-exact, payload 192 byte có thứ tự + EOF chính xác, status con lấy được cả từ `Wait` lẫn từ endpoint, target không review bị từ chối, command line quá dài bị chặn trước kernel, và một lần launch bị từ chối không để lại command line nào.
- kernel/argv: command line đã stage không còn nằm trong map khoá `tid` mà ở hai trường task-local (`Task::staged_argv` cho lần launch kế tiếp của chính task, `Task::inherited_argv` cho con được spawner trao trước khi chạy). Map cũ cho phép cleanup "lần launch thất bại" của một task *khác* xoá argv đang chờ của con — tái hiện được: `install tid=21 len=24` rồi `take tid=21 -> None` cùng địa chỉ virt+phys, không remover nào được ghi nhận, và con báo `argv items=0`; nay lớp lỗi đó bất khả thi theo cấu trúc. Hành vi quan sát được của `StateStash`/`StateRestore` trên key argv giữ nguyên.
- kernel/loader: `SpawnFromPath` bị từ chối nay ghi một dòng WARN nêu caller, path và lý do (`NotFound`/`PermissionDenied`/…), thay vì chỉ trả `-1` cho cell; đây là thứ chỉ ra rằng route path thô không giải được cell trên đĩa vì kernel cố ý không điều khiển block device trên QEMU.
- port-platform/pthread: `cellos_pthread.c` dùng chung thunk syscall trong header nội bộ mới `cellos_syscall.h` thay vì giữ bản sao riêng, để adapter spawn và pthread có đúng một định nghĩa hợp đồng thanh ghi.

- cells/service-httpd: support CLI arguments `<port> [vfs_path]` while retaining default port 8080 and REST/AI routing when run without arguments. `cells/services/httpd/src/router.rs` serves the requested VFS file directly on connection when a path is supplied, unifying the legacy tool use case (`httpd 9091 /tmp/resp.txt &`) with the web server use case (`httpd &`) and resolving `network_httpd_serves_file` and `network_httpd_dynamic_content` in the Network Data-Path suite.
- c2c-oracle: sign isolated cell binaries in `scripts/run-c2c-broker-oracle-qemu.sh` using `lib-sign-cells.sh`. Under ADR-0015, unsigned cells are admitted to Tier 2 Paged Domains whose private `satp` roots lack peripheral MMIO mappings; signing them with the dev key ensures driver cells (`platform`, `block`, `vfs`) are admitted as Tier 1 Trusted SAS cells with full MMIO/DMA access, completely passing the C2C Broker Oracle test.
- cells/service-hypervisor: declare and initialize a 10 MiB custom heap via `ostd::declare_custom_heap!` in `cells/services/hypervisor/src/main.rs`. When `HV_VOLATILE_DISK=1` is set, `virtio_blk.rs` allocates a 4 MiB volatile disk buffer which previously exceeded the default 1 MiB heap, causing `OOM: cell heap exhausted — size=4194304` in an infinite restart loop during x86_64 hypervisor smoke tests.
- cells/tetris-c: the Tetris-OS port no longer depends on an unfetchable gitlink. Its upstream source (Banaxi-Tech/Tetris-OS, MIT, commit `66c4466`) is vendored into `cells/demos/tetris-c/src/c/tetris-os/` with its licence, `build.rs` records the provenance, and the one change we carry — the idle loop's per-ISA wait instruction (`hlt` on x86, `wfi` on RISC-V, because upstream's x86 form does not assemble for riscv64) — is tracked instead of sitting as uncommitted dirt in a nested clone. `gen_disk.ps1` now probes the real path (`src/tetris.c`), so the cell is packaged rather than silently skipped, and the platform shim gained the two hooks upstream actually calls (`keyboard_set_layout`, `speaker_music_update`) so `cargo build -p tetris-c --target x86_64-unknown-none` links end to end. The riscv64 link still needs the xpack picolibc, which is why the CI exclusions keep the cell and their comments now say so.
- kernel/rv64: the trap boundary now holds the root invariant the intermittent `/srv` fault exposed. Trap entry parks the interrupted `satp` in the trap frame — RV64-only, 304 bytes including padding, so the x86_64/aarch64 layouts and their 288-byte frames are untouched — and installs the kernel root from the fixed-name `VI_KERNEL_SATP` symbol before any kernel code runs, so the timer tick's console poll and the PLIC claim never issue an MMIO access under a Cell root. `__trap_exit` restores the frame's root only when the live value differs (a task resumed through a context switch already has it; a primed first-entry frame carries 0), and `SwitchPlan::SameDomain` now programs its domain's root instead of the zero no-write sentinel — leaving it at `(0, 0)` would resume a Cell under the kernel root with the kernel's mappings visible to its S-mode code. Private-domain user copies already branch on the resident root, so they take their stricter PTE-walk alias path inside a handler, and `validate_kernel_range` now walks the kernel root and refuses user addresses passed as kernel buffers. Evidence: 10/10 consecutive clean boots on the phase-03 direct-drive instrument (`posix-shim-test` reaching `POSIX-RENAME: OK`, zero `[KERNEL PANIC]`), 10/10 green `cargo test --test srv-cellosfs` runs, and the RV64 domain suite green on one and two harts with the new `S22-RV64-RESUME-ROOT` fixture plus a `resume-root` case in `scripts/qemu-native-domain-test.sh`. Hosted: the `CellosFS /srv Integration Test` job is green on run 34933281918 (its first green push); the `C2C Broker Oracle` job's boot no longer prints the 487 PLIC-fault kernel exceptions that used to halt it (923 on a local control) — it now reaches Embedded Init and fails on a cell-side fault, which is the C2C lane's own remaining defect; and the `Network Data-Path Integration (riscv64)` suite goes from 4 panicking failures among 6 to 2 failures with zero kernel exceptions (`52 passed; 2 failed`), the remainder being the two HTTP-server tests that failed without a panic before the fix too. `.agents/260914-ci-gate-restoration/phase-04-trap-root-discipline.md`.
- rv64/test-runner: `scripts/qemu-native-domain-test.sh` no longer pins the `SMP-FAULT-RETIREMENT` canary to `generation 99`. That field is `NEXT_DOMAIN` — how many private AddressSpaces had been built when the synthetic trap published its record — and the unmodified kernel reads 133 on this workstation today against 99 in the phase-07 captures, so the pin failed a correct kernel at `--harts 2` (measured on both the pre-change and post-change builds). The canary is now matched by cell, task, injected cause, and null pc/addr, with the measurement recorded in the comment.
- vfs/srv: the RedoxFS retirement is complete end to end. P5 ships raw and the guest formats it as CellosFS Native (no host `redoxfs-ar` build in either image script), the suite, its CI job, its cache key and its labels are `srv-cellosfs`, ADR 0016 supersedes ADR 09b and marks ADR 0002's backend as historical while its G1/G2 policy survives, and the remaining RedoxFS labels in kernel/VFS comments plus the orphaned `backend_stub.rs` are gone. Nothing in the workspace depends on `third_party/redoxfs`; it stays vendored for reference. The job's red state at that point was the kernel trap-root fault, not the filesystem — closed by the trap-boundary fix above.
- kernel/rv64: the kernel-fault panic prints the live `satp` beside the kernel's own, and the kernel records that root when it activates its page table. This is the instrument that proved the intermittent `console_drv::poll` fault (UART line-status load at `0x10000005`, `scause=13`) runs with a private Cell root live — `satp` ASID 1 — instead of the kernel root's ASID 0, because the trap entry never restores the kernel root. `hal/arch/riscv/src/rv64/{trap,domain,paging}.rs`; the fix is designed in `.agents/260914-ci-gate-restoration/phase-04-trap-root-discipline.md`.
- ai/rpi3: the inference stack runs on the physical Raspberry Pi 3 over the existing static-TFTP netboot lane. The fixture's golden ids and embedding reproduce on real Cortex-A53 (`ai-test` PASS, 8 tokens in 59 ms), and `tools/rpi3-netboot/serve-ai-oracle.ps1` boots a chosen payload and captures the board's console. The checkpoint no longer has to live inside the kernel: `service-ai` reads `/bin/ai-model.gguf` and then `/mnt/sd/ai-model.gguf` (the card's own FAT volume) and logs the path it used, and `build-aarch64-cells.ps1 -AiCells` builds the AI cells with no checkpoint at all — the 43.6 MB embedded-payload variant panics the board's kernel at compositor setup while the same image passes on QEMU aarch64 virt.
- ai/oracle: the inference oracle now runs on **both** ISA legs of Spec 24 CP-3's gate. `scripts/run-ai-inference-oracle-qemu.sh --arch riscv64|aarch64` builds, signs, and boots the same isolated image on either architecture, the CI job is a two-leg matrix, and each evidence artifact names the architecture and model it ran against. On aarch64 (a softfloat target, so its float arithmetic is software) the fixture's golden ids and embedding reproduce exactly, and a real 26.7 MB checkpoint generates coherent prose — a numerics cross-check of the integer Q8_0 kernels on a second ISA, not just a second boot test.
- ai-engine/tensor-math: **4.3× faster inference in the Cell, 1.8× on the host.** Activations are now quantized to Q8_0 once per projection and each 32-weight block is an exact integer dot product scaled by `d_w·d_a`, instead of decoding every block to f32 and running 32 f32 multiply-accumulates. ### 🐛 Fixes
- cells/unsafe-allowlist: the three C port-platform witnesses (`cells/tests/c-pthread`, `c-spawn`, `c-spawn-child`) were committed without F1/F5 exemptions, so the signing gate refused with `REFUSED: not signing — the F1/F5 check above did not pass` and **every** test-hooks lane stopped building (`build-test-hooks-ci.sh` → `qemu-native-domain-test.sh`, the aarch64/x86 hooks lanes). Each file's `unsafe extern "C"` block is the only way to call its linked C witness, so they are allowlisted as `c-ffi` (three `[[file]]` entries plus the matching `[[crate]]` entries). Verified: the gate now reports `OK: signed 8 cell(s) with the dev key after a passing F1/F5 check`.
- kernel/audit: the audit ring no longer loses records when two harts log at once. `log_event` read `head`, wrote its bytes, and stored the advanced `head` without any cross-hart exclusion — the module doc said the interrupt guard was "safe on single-hart" — so two harts could claim the same byte range and both store the same advanced cursor, silently overwriting one record; the ring carries security events (`CellSpawnDenied`, `CellSignatureFailed`, `SyscallDenied`). S-mode interrupts stay disabled (which also keeps the new lock unreachable from an ISR, so it cannot self-deadlock) and a `WRITE` lock now serialises producers, so a record is written before its cursor is published and `head` always advances by whole records.
- kernel/atomic-publication tests: `AP-13` reported a missing observation as a contract failure. The fixture asks the RT hart to inspect ready-queue visibility from inside a scheduler entry while the publisher owns `SCHEDULER`; when that hart did not reach the probe inside the bounded spin budget the observation never happened, and the case failed — measured on `--harts 2` as FAIL/PASS/PASS with one build, and FAIL again on 2026-09-18 before this program's kernel changes. A probe build showed every other conjunct true (`task/next_id/ready/quota/measurements/evidence`), i.e. the publication itself was correct in every failing run. `competing_hart_schedule_attempt` now returns `Some(true)`/`Some(false)`/`None`, and `None` logs `ATOMIC_PUBLICATION_AP-13: SKIP (competing hart did not reach a scheduler entry)` instead of failing; an observed early visibility is still a hard FAIL. The same change drops the `audit_delta.is_multiple_of(18)` rule, which cannot hold on two harts because the ring interleaves records of different lengths (the probe measured a 54-byte delta = three records) — the rule is now "at least two records' worth of evidence and nothing dropped while publishing", with `AP-15` keeping its exact `== 36`.

- scripts/qemu-futex-test: the futex witness runner no longer reports a missing PASS marker for a cell that is still working. The witness needs ~68 s from the command to its marker on this workstation (10 000 serialised futex increments plus 2 000 two-thread ping-pong rounds) while the runner waited 45 s inside a 90 s QEMU window — measured: 4 of 6 runs reported `FAIL: futex-test did not reach its PASS marker` with the cell healthy, and a long-window run reaches PASS in 68 s with every phase correct. The boot and marker windows are now 180 s and 150 s, both env-overridable (`BOOT_WINDOW`, `MARKER_WINDOW`). Verified: `FUTEX-TEST-QEMU: PASS` on harts 1 twice and harts 2 once.

- cells/futex-test: the phase-04 futex witness builds again. `fail()` was declared `-> !` while its body only recorded the failure and printed — and the arms that call it sit beside `()` arms (`Ok(0) => println!(…)`), so the declaration, not the body, was wrong; `cargo build -p app-futex-test` died with `E0308: expected !, found ()` and `scripts/qemu-futex-test.sh` could not run at all. The helper now returns, which is what its `FAILURES` counter and the tail's `sys_exit(1)` already assumed. Verified: `FUTEX-TEST-QEMU: PASS` on harts 1 and 2.
