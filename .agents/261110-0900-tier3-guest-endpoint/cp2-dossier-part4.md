The connection only ends when QEMU is killed (`host-ss-after-kill.txt` has no sockets).

## 6. Classification of the cancel finding

**Product defect, confirmed (O1): a Ctrl-C cancel of `cellbox ssh` kills the client cell without any network close, and the Net service never reclaims the dead owner's socket, so the TCP connection, the guest sshd session and the producer stay alive until the Net cell or QEMU ends.** It is not a lane artifact, not a slirp/hostfwd/NIC-bridge problem (the FIN path works end to end in ms: control), not sshd/`yes` semantics (a FIN stops the producer), not the helper (pid arithmetic and `diag pid=` lines). The marker `ssh-guest-cancel-producer` stays **not met** at this ceiling until a product slice (cancel-diagnosis §7.1: kernel watch policy for the Net cell, owner-death delivery to a polling receiver, `abort()` with a kept socket in `release_owner`) lands; that slice needs its own CP1 and handoff (draft §7, D-DIAG-3 A). The other-host Docker lanes' `ssh-cancel-exec` evidence is client-side only and the same Net defect applies (cancel-diagnosis §4, D-DIAG-4 A: note it, edit no record now).
