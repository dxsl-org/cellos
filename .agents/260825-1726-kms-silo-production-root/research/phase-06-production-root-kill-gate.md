# Phase 6 Production-Root Kill Gate

**Decision date:** 2026-08-26  
**Decision:** **NO-GO — no production root product is selected.**  
**Scope:** Evidence-only architecture and product gate; no implementation, test, board, procurement, or provisioning approval.

## Evidence labels

- **VERIFIED** — stated directly by a cited official or standards-body primary source.
- **UNVERIFIED** — a required property is not established by the reviewed official public evidence. This is a gap, not proof that the property is impossible or absent in private/vendor material.
- **CONTESTED** — official evidence supports a broad claim, but another official source contradicts its use here or the evidence does not bind the claim to an exact orderable product/revision.

## Verdict

Issue a hard production-root **NO-GO**. Nuvoton publicly lists the masked OpenTitan family `NPCR1xxxxxBX` as **Mass Production**, and Google says Nuvoton OpenTitan silicon ships in commercial Chromebooks; however, lowRISC explicitly says the Nuvoton Earl Grey chips **“are not open-market parts.”** For the Nuvoton/OpenTitan lane, the reviewed public evidence does not establish an exact orderable MPN/package/revision, product-bound ROM/ROM_EXT/application/cryptolib baseline, provisioning and lifecycle service, support horizon, Cellos content-enforcing command protocol, authenticated wall time, complete rollback-resistant atomic state contract, or approved board integration. Exact TPM and secure-element products are reviewed below, but no reviewed candidate jointly satisfies every mandatory gate ([Nuvoton product page](https://www.nuvoton.com/products/cloud-computing/security/open-titan/), [Google production announcement](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2026/03/opentitan-shipping-in-production.html), [lowRISC product status](https://lowrisc.org/opentitan/)).

The existing Raspberry Pi 3 Model B is independently disqualifying: BCM2837 ROM loads `bootcode.bin` from a configured boot source before code on the AP could report a measurement to a header-attached RoT, while Raspberry Pi's supported customer secure-boot chain begins with Raspberry Pi 4 or newer. SPI0 on J8 is not in the Pi 3 SD boot-media path, so an AP-supplied digest or readiness assertion would be circular trust ([Raspberry Pi boot sequence](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html#boot-sequence), [Raspberry Pi secure boot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/raspberrypi/usbboot/master/docs/secure-boot.md), [Pi 3 Model B schematic, RP-008340-DS](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008340-DS)).

**No product is selected. No purchase, sample order, schematic commitment, vendor NRE, OTP programming, lifecycle transition, personalization, owner transfer, debug lock, RMA-token creation, or other irreversible procurement/provisioning action is approved.** Production is `BLOCKED_BY_ADR_0006`, and production Phases 7–8 remain blocked. Phase 4 remains product-independent software work, blocked only on real protected persistence, authenticated time, and a distinct reviewed pending-key binding. The fail-closed software results from Phases 1–3 remain valid and must not be weakened or represented as production-root qualification.

## Exact evidence evaluation

### 1. Product identity, procurement and support

1. **VERIFIED:** Nuvoton's live product page exposes only the masked family `NPCR1xxxxxBX`, Mass Production status, interfaces, package families and temperature range; it does not expose a complete order code on that page ([Nuvoton OpenTitan](https://www.nuvoton.com/products/cloud-computing/security/open-titan/)).
2. **VERIFIED:** production existence is corroborated by Google's statement that the first Nuvoton OpenTitan part ships in commercially available Chromebooks ([Google, 2026-03-04](https://opensource.googleblog.com/2026/03/opentitan-shipping-in-production.html)).
3. **CONTESTED:** Nuvoton previously described an open-market intent, but the current lowRISC product page says the Nuvoton Earl Grey chips used in Google products/services **“are not open-market parts”** ([Nuvoton, 2024-05-30](https://www.nuvoton.com/news/news/all/TSNuvotonNews-000514/), [lowRISC](https://lowrisc.org/opentitan/)). Production existence therefore must not be treated as ordinary component procurement.
4. **UNVERIFIED:** no reviewed official public evidence binds `NPCR100T`, Earl Grey A2, `Earlgrey-PROD-M6`, or the masked catalog family to one complete saleable MPN/package/die revision. No exact Nuvoton/OpenTitan part is selectable by inference.
5. **UNVERIFIED:** Nuvoton's Product Longevity Program promises longevity only for listed products; the current public PLP list contains no identified NPCR/OpenTitan entry. A component support horizon therefore is not established ([PLP policy](https://www.nuvoton.com/product-related-information/product-longevity-program/), [PLP product list](https://www.nuvoton.com/export/sites/nuvoton/files/PLP_ProductList.pdf)).
6. **UNVERIFIED:** public-store searches reviewed on 2026-08-26 returned no OpenTitan product, but a dry store search is only absence of public purchasing evidence, not proof that NDA/direct procurement is impossible ([Nuvoton Direct](https://direct.nuvoton.com/en/search?controller=search&orderby=position&orderway=desc&search_query=OpenTitan&submit_search=), [TECHDesign](https://www.techdesign.com/market/search?search=OpenTitan&s=false&r=false&utm_source=nuvoton&utm_medium=supplier%20referral&utm_campaign=nuvoton%20shop)).

**Gate result: FAIL.** “Mass Production” and deployment establish existence, not an exact purchasable and supportable production configuration.

### 2. Firmware, cryptolib and protocol

1. **VERIFIED design evidence:** `Earlgrey-PROD-M6` records final production-tapeout physical design; the earlier M5 release described final production ROM, while M6 later recorded an immutable ROM/ROM_EXT-section correction. This makes exact release-to-product binding mandatory ([M5](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/releases/tag/Earlgrey-PROD-M5), [M6](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/releases/tag/Earlgrey-PROD-M6)).
2. **UNVERIFIED product evidence:** Nuvoton's public product presentation does not identify mask-ROM hash, ROM_EXT/application image, cryptolib version, build configuration, update/recovery policy or signed release manifest ([Nuvoton OpenTitan](https://www.nuvoton.com/products/cloud-computing/security/open-titan/)). Upstream source and development bundles must not be assumed to be shipped vendor firmware.
3. **VERIFIED:** OpenTitan's public P-256 signing API accepts a caller-supplied 32-byte pre-hashed digest and places hash-security responsibility on the caller; it does not define CSR or TLS parameters ([OpenTitan `ecc_p256.h`](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/blob/earlgrey_1.0.0/sw/device/lib/crypto/include/ecc_p256.h)).
4. **VERIFIED standard requirement:** PKCS#10 signs the DER-encoded `CertificationRequestInfo`, not an arbitrary digest supplied without semantic validation ([RFC 2986 §4](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc2986#section-4)). TLS 1.3 CertificateVerify signs 64 spaces, a role-specific context string, a zero byte and the transcript hash ([RFC 8446 §4.4.3](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8446#section-4.4.3)).
5. **VERIFIED transport-only evidence:** Earl Grey SPI_DEVICE can pass firmware-visible command metadata/payload, but its documented uploaded payload is bounded and its TPM-over-SPI block leaves command/address decoding to firmware; it is not evidence of the required application protocol ([SPI_DEVICE theory of operation](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/spi_device/doc/theory_of_operation.html)).
6. **UNVERIFIED:** no reviewed official public Nuvoton/OpenTitan production command set reconstructs and validates both required contents or proves all generic-sign and alternate-firmware routes unreachable. A custom owner image may be technically conceivable; until bound to an exact supported product and negative vectors, it is a new qualification project rather than evidence for selection.

**Gate result: FAIL.** Protected key storage plus generic ECDSA is not a content-enforcing production root.

### 3. Immutable AP↔RoT boot authorization

1. **VERIFIED:** Raspberry Pi's BCM2837 boot description has ROM choose an OTP-enabled source, load `bootcode.bin` and jump to it; it also documents unsigned USB device boot behavior. Its supported customer secure-boot tooling is for Raspberry Pi 4 or newer ([boot sequence](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html#boot-sequence), [USB device boot](https://www.raspberrypi.com/documentation/computers/raspberry-pi.html#usb-device-boot-mode), [secure boot](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/raspberrypi/usbboot/master/docs/secure-boot.md)).
2. **VERIFIED:** the Pi 3 Model B schematic exposes SPI0 on J8 GPIO7–11 and RUN separately, while SD uses other SoC signals; the header SPI path does not mediate the ROM's boot-media reads ([RP-008340-DS](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/documents/RP-008340-DS)).
3. **VERIFIED reference capability, inapplicable topology:** Earl Grey SPI passthrough and `sysrst_ctrl` reset control can support architectures where the RoT is designed into boot flash/reset paths. They do not establish electrical compatibility with Pi 3 RUN or move J8 SPI0 into the Pi 3 boot path ([SPI_DEVICE](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/spi_device/doc/theory_of_operation.html), [`sysrst_ctrl`](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/sysrst_ctrl/doc/theory_of_operation.html)).
4. **VERIFIED architecture result:** on the existing Pi 3, unverified AP code runs before it can send a measurement over header SPI. Trusting that AP-supplied measurement, pairing state, `attested=true`, or boot-complete signal would let a compromised AP self-authorize.

**Gate result: FAIL.** An SD interposer, modified PCB, or replacement AP could define a separate future board lane, but none is evidence for the current board and none is approved here.

### 4. Rollback-resistant state and power-loss behavior

1. **VERIFIED reference mechanism:** OpenTitan boot-data software uses two flash-info pages, a monotonic counter and minimum security versions; it reads back a new entry before invalidating the old one because writes can be interrupted ([`boot_data.h`](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/blob/earlgrey_1.0.0/sw/device/silicon_creator/lib/boot_data.h), [`boot_data.c`](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/blob/earlgrey_1.0.0/sw/device/silicon_creator/lib/boot_data.c)).
2. **VERIFIED limitation:** the reference code notes that counter wraparound is not checked. Flash documentation warns that power removal during program/erase may damage the flash macro and leaves actual macro behavior vendor-defined ([`boot_data.c`](https://github.com/lowRISC/opentitan/blob/earlgrey_1.0.0/sw/device/silicon_creator/lib/boot_data.c), [flash controller](https://opentitan.org/book/hw/ip/flash_ctrl/doc/theory_of_operation.html)).
3. **VERIFIED:** OTP supports lockable partitions and irreversible lifecycle/provisioning use, but OTP words cannot be treated as an indefinitely advancing application transaction store ([OTP controller](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/otp_ctrl/doc/theory_of_operation.html), [OTP programmer's guide](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/otp_ctrl/doc/programmers_guide.html)).
4. **UNVERIFIED:** no product evidence supplies one authenticated, anti-replay, anti-wrap, power-loss-atomic commit covering complete profile, chain, verifier set, denylist, qualification tuple, key generation, firmware and authenticated-time floors, including endurance/exhaustion and recovery semantics.

**Gate result: FAIL.** A generic boot-data pattern is not a qualification of the exact product flash macro or the required Cellos-wide state transition.

### 5. Authenticated wall time

1. **VERIFIED:** the Earl Grey feature inventory documents fixed-frequency and always-on timers, not a secure RTC or authenticated civil-time source ([Earl Grey datasheet](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/top_earlgrey/doc/datasheet.html)).
2. **VERIFIED:** the AON wake timer is firmware-configurable/clearable, has non-atomic 64-bit access and halts in the killed state; it does not preserve an authenticated epoch across power loss ([AON timer theory](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/aon_timer/doc/theory_of_operation.html), [AON timer programmer's guide](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/hw/ip/aon_timer/doc/programmers_guide.html)).
3. **UNVERIFIED:** Nuvoton publishes no product secure RTC, battery-backed authenticated epoch, signed-time service, nonce/freshness protocol, outage rule or rollback-resistant time-floor contract.
4. **VERIFIED mismatch:** a TPM logical clock and `ClockSet` are not authenticated civil wall time ([TCG `ClockSet.c`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/TrustedComputingGroup/TPM/main/TPMCmd/tpm/src/command/ClockTimer/ClockSet.c)).

**Gate result: FATAL FAIL.** An independently signed time authority could define a future architecture, but that adds another production trust root and requires a pinned protocol, outage semantics and protected nondecreasing floor; none is approved by current evidence.

### 6. Board, provisioning, lifecycle, RMA and revocation

1. **VERIFIED reference design:** Earl Grey lifecycle defines RAW/TEST/DEV/PROD/PROD_END/RMA/SCRAP states, token-gated transitions and debug restrictions; this is generic design behavior, not evidence of the state, tokens or services of an exact Nuvoton product ([device lifecycle](https://opentitan.org/earlgrey_1.0.0/book/doc/security/specs/device_life_cycle/index.html)).
2. **VERIFIED reference design:** public manufacturing firmware describes EMPTY→INITIALIZED→INDIVIDUALIZED→PERSONALIZED stages, but does not establish Nuvoton factory stations, custody, interruption recovery, acceptance records or product images ([manufacturing firmware](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lowRISC/opentitan/earlgrey_1.0.0/sw/device/silicon_creator/manuf/README.md)).
3. **UNVERIFIED:** no public exact-product pinout/netlist establishes SPI CS, IRQ/ready, reset, power-good/brownout, tamper, JTAG/lifecycle straps, voltage domains, pull-ups, timing, back-power prevention, signal integrity or independent reset behavior for a Cellos board.
4. **UNVERIFIED:** no vendor procedure defines the exact OTP map and power-loss boundaries, idempotency, readback, duplicate-ID prevention, quarantine/scrap, token custody, owner transfer, RMA wipe, post-RMA serve denial or destructive zeroization.
5. **UNVERIFIED:** no operational package binds one signed qualification record to the exact RoT/AP/board/firmware/protocol/profile tuple and revokes NodeId/certificate/qualification before RMA, loss or destruction.

**Gate result: FAIL.** No schematic or provisioning run is approvable from generic design documentation.

| “A monotonic timer/counter is enough.” | **VERIFIED:** AON timer, TPM clock and boot-data counter facilities exist. | None is authenticated civil time, and none publicly establishes one atomic full-policy record on the exact product. Adjustable/cold-boot behavior and unchecked counter wrap defeat the proposed substitution. |
| “No public advisory means the candidate is secure.” | Some reviewed product searches were dry. | A dry search is **UNVERIFIED disclosure evidence**, never proof of absence. Without exact MPN/revision/firmware, advisories and errata cannot be mapped to the deployed TCB. |

**Refutation result:** no reviewed candidate jointly satisfies the content-enforcing command, immutable AP boot, protected state, authenticated time, exact board/provisioning, and procurement/support gates. Specific public mismatches are recorded above; unavailable public evidence remains **UNVERIFIED**, not proof that a private capability is absent. The NO-GO stands without weakening any requirement.

## Vendor evidence required to reopen

Reopening requires a single coherent, vendor-signed evidence package bound to one exact product. NDA material may support evaluation, but the decision record must retain immutable hashes/identifiers and auditable conclusions for the shipped configuration.

1. **Product and supply:** complete MPN/order code, package, top marking, die/mask/metal revision, manufacturing site/traceability, errata and PCN baseline; authorized quote/listing, MOQ, lead time, unrestricted availability, lifecycle class, last-order/last-ship terms, security-contact SLA and dated support/update horizon.
2. **Pinned TCB:** mask-ROM hash, ROM_EXT/application/cryptolib version and hash, build configuration, exact source commit, signing keys/certificates, signed release manifest, certification scope, update/recovery/rescue policy, anti-rollback rules and applicable errata.
3. **Typed command ABI:** versioned transport and canonical schemas; positive/negative vectors proving protected reconstruction and validation of PKCS#10 CRI and both permitted TLS 1.3 CertificateVerify roles; proof that caller-body/digest/raw/test signing, generic TPM signing and downgrade/alternate-image paths are unreachable.
4. **AP binding:** exact immutable chain showing how the RoT observes or controls first-stage AP boot, verifies the authorized measurement before release, binds the physical AP/RoT pair and refuses self-asserted AP readiness.
5. **Protocol security:** endpoint identities and keys; challenge/session establishment, reset epochs, monotonic sequencing, replay windows, complete request/response authentication, purpose/measurement/qualification binding, version negotiation without downgrade, timeout and fail-closed behavior.
6. **Protected state:** product-qualified authenticated and rollback-resistant storage with one atomic record for firmware, profile, certificate chain, verifier set, denylist, qualification, policy/key generation and time floors; torn-write recovery, endurance, anti-wrap, exhaustion and terminal behavior; power-cut evidence at every commit point.
7. **Authenticated time:** secure RTC evidence or independently authenticated nonce-bound signed-time service, authority and key-rotation rules, freshness/skew/outage semantics, protected nondecreasing floor and recovery after prolonged loss of power/network.
8. **Entropy and cryptography:** product entropy-source characterization/validation, approved production configuration and thresholds, fail-closed health behavior, P-256 side-channel/fault countermeasures, key derivation/sideload/non-exportability evidence and cryptographic errata.
9. **Provisioning/lifecycle:** exact lifecycle state and OTP configuration; factory HSM/station authentication; key/identity ceremonies and custody; owner transfer; debug/test lock; readback/attestation; interruption resume-versus-scrap rules; duplicate-ID prevention; quarantine and destruction records.
10. **Board package:** exact schematic/netlist/BOM and pinout for boot media, SPI, IRQ/ready, reset, dedicated/independent power, power-good/brownout, levels/pull-ups, signal integrity, mux/isolation, debug straps, tamper/enclosure, RMA access and destruction; vendor electrical/security review.
11. **Qualification and operations:** independently signed per-device record binding AP board/revision/boot measurement and RoT product/revision/lifecycle/TCB/protocol/profile/KMS/provider/image/policy/key generation; non-transferability; relay/CA denylist and immediate-revocation flow; lost/stolen/RMA/scrap/replacement audit procedure.
12. **Exact-board evidence:** substitution, replay, malformed/stalled bus, IRQ loss/storm, independent reset, brownout at each state/provisioning transition, boot bypass, downgrade, debug/RMA, post-RMA denial, tamper and destructive-zeroization results on the production assembly—not QEMU, FPGA, an evaluation board or a jumper-wire prototype.

A vendor package that qualifies only the silicon cannot repair the Pi 3 boot topology. Reopening on Pi 3 additionally requires a separately approved board architecture that places the RoT in the immutable boot path; otherwise a different AP/board lane is mandatory.

## Decision consequences

- **No product selection:** `NPCR1xxxxxBX`, `NPCR100T`, Earl Grey A2/M6, TPMs, secure elements, Titan, Pluton and Caliptra remain candidates or references only.
- **No irreversible action:** no procurement commitment or OTP/lifecycle/provisioning operation is authorized.
- **Production remains fail-closed:** production is `BLOCKED_BY_ADR_0006`, and Phases 7–8 cannot advance; there is no fallback to QEMU, FPGA, Silo, raw/K1, TPM generic sign, or a weaker content contract. Phase 4 remains product-independent software work under its three entry gates and is not part of this hardware block.
- **Software evidence remains correctly scoped:** Phases 1–3 remain useful software-complete evidence, not hardware qualification.
- **Future reopening is fresh qualification:** vendor-private availability or a custom firmware/applet/board is not a clerical update; it requires a new evidence review against every gate above.
