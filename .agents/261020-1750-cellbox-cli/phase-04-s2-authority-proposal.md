## 1. Key finding
The shell already runs its builtins in-process under its own ceiling (which contains `GetProcs*`, `MemInfo`, raw `Open/Read/ReadDir`, VFS IPC, `WaitCompletion`, …); S1 kept that ceiling identical, so moving the shell into `cellbox` widened nothing. A real **widening** can only come from moving the *separate* ELFs into the one artifact: `kill` (ambient `SpawnCap` + `ForceExit`, `kernel/src/task/syscall.rs:5581-5647`), `shutdown` (raw syscall 502, no gate, `:4133-4151,7821-7862`), `hotswap`/`snapshot` (SupervisorCap, authorization by mutable task name, `cells/services/supervisor/src/main.rs:52-93`), network tools (net service has attestation and socket ownership but no per-invocation DNS/destination/port grant, `cells/services/net/src/handlers/tcp.rs:120-164`) and the `nc -l`/`httpd`/`mqtt` listeners. Plan step 3 says exactly: if a right cannot be enforced narrowly, keep the effect behind a **separate worker**, never widen every invocation.

## 2. S2-lite — what this proposal would approve (no ABI, kernel, selector or service change)
1. **Disposition ledger (docs + registry data, no behavior change).** Every catalog command gets one of two final dispositions, recorded in `command-catalog.md` and enforced by a test that diffs the registry against the ledger so nothing can be silently lost or silently joined:
   - **In-suite (runs inside the cellbox ceiling):** all shell builtins and state/control commands, text/typed transforms, `echo help uname env date uptime sleep`, and the builtin `ls cat ps free top wc head tail sort uniq tee mkdir rmdir rm touch cp mv` implementations that already run inside the shell ceiling (VFS enforces caller/path policy; telemetry rows are read through the already-held `GetProcs*`/`MemInfo`).
   - **Retained independent workers (never in the suite ELF; own launch edge and ceiling as today):** `kill`, `shutdown`, `hotswap`, `snapshot`, hypervisor start, `curl`, `wget`, `nc`, `ping`, `ifconfig/ip`, `httpd`, `mqtt`, `blktest` (expected-denial diagnostic), and the sys-tools/net-tools ELFs generally. They stay buildable/signable/packagable; the plan's "retain independent servers/workers" clause covers them, and **no CLI table parser or fake output** replaces them.
2. **Denial proof on the private signed image** (extends the Phase 03 runner; observed results only): from the cellbox shell (a restricted caller edge) attempt each ungranted right and require a refusal with no effect: spawn of a non-reviewed path; `/bin/kill`, `/bin/shutdown`, `/bin/hotswap`, `/bin/snapshot` edges (S1 already covers kill/shutdown/old shell); raw block (`blktest`) denied; a forged argv selecting a privileged mode in a retained worker launched through an unreviewed edge is refused at the launch edge; telemetry/`free` rows come from the held syscalls only; network tools launched through their reviewed edges still need the net service (observed), while no listener can be started by the suite itself. A refusal must be a real denial (launch-edge `DENY` log or typed `Denied`), not a missing file.
3. **Registry test:** `cellbox` dispatch (feature `applets`) and the ledger agree; a command in both lists fails the test.

## 3. What S2-lite deliberately does NOT do (each needs its own CP1/ABI proposal before it may move into the suite)
- **S2b VFS scoped mutation** (replace the ambient `VfsMutate` task bit by per-operation destination handles) — `libs/api` + VFS service + kernel attestation change.
- **S2c Lifecycle owner** (`KillTarget` bound to Cell generation, `ResetSystem` via platform provider, `SnapshotTarget`, `ReplaceService`) — supervisor/kernel ABI.
- **S2d Net scoped handles** (DNS/destination/port/listen grants) — net service ABI.
- **S2e Telemetry owner** (scoped observation reference replacing global `GetProcs`) — kernel/D04.
Until those exist the retained workers keep their effects behind their own ELF boundaries, which is the plan's explicit fallback. Phase 04 criterion 3 ("every cataloged ungranted right is denied … under restricted caller edges") is therefore met only to the extent of what S2-lite's image denials demonstrate; the scoped-handle criteria remain open and stay unchecked.
