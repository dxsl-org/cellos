**a5. Guest networking and reachability.**
- Guest NIC: virtio-mmio net at `0xd0000200`/IRQ6 (`.agents/260827-1004-hardware-independent-roadmap/phase-10-tier3-x86-virtio-parity.md:68`), MAC `GUEST_MAC 52:54:00:AA:BB:CC` (`virtio_net.rs:13`). Backend `net_backend.rs`: raw L2 frames to the Net Cell via `NetRequest::L2Send`/`L2Recv`, right `Right::L2` (`net/src/rights.rs:60`), hypervisor policy row has `NET_L2` (`scripts/sign-policy.py:128`).
- Net Cell treats the guest as a learned-MAC L2 port next to its own smoltcp stack: `interface.rs:576-609` `route_rx` queues a frame to the guest only if its dst MAC is the learned guest MAC or broadcast, otherwise to smoltcp; `handlers.rs:200-209` + `interface.rs:376-396` `send_l2` puts the guest frame on the NIC driver TX queue. There is no internal loopback or switch between the guest and the Cellos IP stack in that code. So a Cellos cell (cellbox-ssh, `NET_CLIENT` = resolve+connect, `sign-policy.py:134`) reaches the guest only over the external wire. Under QEMU user-mode networking `[INFERENCE]` slirp does not hairpin one NIC's two MACs; a route via host loopback `hostfwd` into a second slirp lease is conceivable but unproven and nothing in the repo does it.
- Prior evidence of guest traffic: x86 parity phase says "bounded network smoke" with DHCP/apk-style evidence under TCG 10.2.0 (same doc :45,:70-76). Not re-read beyond that.
- Cellbox's client supports password auth (D79-6, phase-09-ssh-client.md:58); `--auth publickey` is refused (:100). A password guest endpoint therefore does not need KMS.
