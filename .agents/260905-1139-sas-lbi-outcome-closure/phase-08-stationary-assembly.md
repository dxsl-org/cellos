# Phase 08: ASSEMBLY-01 Stationary Coupling and Lab Operation

> **Required — deviation-log:** Record every Decision / Deviation / Surprise immediately in § Deviation Log; use the smallest reversible option and escalate irreversible or contract-breaking changes.

## Overview
Couple and decouple independently specified upper/lab and base modules while stationary, verify configuration/locks/power/safety, and require explicit local re-enable. The user-visible outcome includes LAB-01 transferring an identified dry inert closed dummy carrier from indexed rack A to indexed rack B in the assembled stationary configuration, plus demonstrated independent upper/lab and base use—not just successful module discovery.

## Requirements
- ASSEMBLY-01 covers stationary mechanical/electrical coupling and decoupling only. No hot docking, automatic motion after reconnect, walking/jumping/stairs, balance algorithm, full ROS/NPU port, hazardous samples, precision claims without metrology or military/swarm behavior.
- Preserve three use cases: upper/lab alone performs LAB-01; base alone performs BASE-01 secured-tray handoff; assembled and stationary performs LAB-01 with the base immobilized in its approved configuration. Distinct support/power/control/safety requirements must be met for each; separability alone proves none of them.
- LAB-01 means one dry inert closed dummy carrier with ID moved one-at-a-time from indexed source rack A to indexed destination rack B, authoritative actual placement observed and traceable result retained. No pipetting, opening carriers, autonomous experiment selection or substitutions for uncertain samples.
- One active workflow job shared across LAB-01, BASE-01 and ASSEMBLY-01; composed steps retain its identity, and other requests are rejected until completion or explicit reconciliation closes it. Immutable fixture/configuration identity per run and bounded job/carrier/source/destination identity. No coupling transition with an active LAB/BASE substep or unsecured/uncertain carrier: enter reconciliation and apply the approved retention procedure before proceeding.
- Lab-arm activity and base mobility are mutually exclusive. Stationary/support/lock eligibility must remain fresh and valid from LAB admission until carrier retention and arm parking. Mid-job loss invokes immediate independent inhibition and the approved safe disposition, retains carrier custody and produces `Indeterminate/ReconcileRequired`; explicit reconciliation and re-enable are required before either mode resumes. This is not simultaneous mobile manipulation or a software-response-time safety guarantee.
- 08A drafting can start under WIP; implementation/acceptance consumes accepted 06A+07A contracts. 08B consumes native software witnesses, not missing robots. 08C remains external-gated and unexercised until exact components and coupling readiness exist. Full ASSEMBLY-01 stays incomplete without 08C.

## Architecture
- **Source facts:** `cells/tests/bench/src/main.rs:191-218` dispatches private bench roles and `cells/tests/bench/src/scenarios.rs:11-22` separates scenarios. Reuse that shape and Phase06/07 contracts; no generic modular-robot framework.
- **Source facts:** `libs/ostd/src/fs.rs:52-81` owns VFS file capabilities and caches provider identity until reopen; `tests/integration/tests/redoxfs-srv.rs:5-17` describes native RV64 persistent-image witnesses. Reuse Phase05's actual backend/restart/oracles; persisted records are not physical assembly or sample-state evidence.
- **Source facts:** `boards/raspberry-pi/3-model-b/board.rs:6-24,46-62` declares a Model B platform/peripheral inventory, not a module connector, motor drive, lock sensor or safety controller. `cells/demos/robot-demo/src/main.rs:77-112` has synthetic sensor fallback and unchecked GPIO results; its done marker cannot authorize physical coupling or prove LAB-01.
- **Reported context:** Only QEMU and two owner-reported Pi3 B+ development boards are available; robot/modules/fixtures/actuators/safety hardware and procurement are unselected. Board identity reconciliation and all exact-device gates remain separate from software model development.
- Logical flow: quiesce/retain → establish independently safe supported state → isolate prescribed energy → couple or decouple → observe mechanical locks, electrical/power and safety configuration → invalidate old requests/control state → reconcile physical/sample state → explicit local re-enable → admit the eligible mode.
- Configuration is immutable during a run; any module, connection, fixture, payload-support or controller change ends that run and invalidates outstanding requests, cached control/trajectory state and old-generation observations. Reconnection must not resume an old LAB-01 or BASE-01 command.
- A bounded coordinator tracks intent and observations, not physical truth by assertion. Command accepted, connector detected and software ready are each insufficient: physical locks/support, electrical condition and local safety eligibility need authoritative observations fixed by the exact package.
- Partial coupling, lock disagreement, wrong module identity, power loss, host failure or observation loss leaves operation inhibited in the independently defined safe disposition. An in-flight carrier or uncertain physical outcome becomes `Indeterminate/ReconcileRequired`; no blind replay, reassignment or inverse movement. Retain job/carrier attribution through operator reconciliation.
- Independent local mechanisms must retain/support/isolate safely with Cellos/Linux failed; loss of software or electrical power must not be assumed to release a brake or latch safely. Coupled modules share mechanical/energy hazards; separate software instances cannot establish hazard independence.
- Shared records are private logical contracts only, not a public ABI/wire format. Local native QEMU witnesses remain on one node; no cross-board SAS pointer, assumed shared memory, remote/public C2C bypass or ad-hoc network fallback.

2. Implement a deterministic private assembly plant model whose support/lock/power/safety and carrier placement observations are separate from requested state. Model successful independent LAB-01, independent BASE-01 and assembled stationary LAB-01; label all outcomes model-only.

   The 08A, 08B and hazard-owner-authorized 08C failure matrices must include stationary/support/lock eligibility loss during manipulation, after admission but before carrier retention/arm parking. Show independent inhibition, retained attribution, reconciliation and no automatic resumption; physical injection stays within the approved safe procedure.
4. **08B — native QEMU witness; requires 06B + 07B software contracts/witnesses, NOT physical 06C/07C.** Compose their native bounded scenarios with assembly transitions and real VFS result/readback. Run independent modes and assembled stationary LAB-01; inject software-side configuration/restart/stale-identity cases through existing private fixture conventions. Observe modeled actual carrier placement, not merely enum transitions or demo banners; record exact image/profile and failures.
5. **08C readiness package — external-gated.** Require accepted 06C and 07C evidence for the exact components plus a separate accountable coupling hazard-owner review. Pin module/board/controller identities; mechanical mounts, locking and load paths; standalone and combined stability/support/centre-of-mass; connector pinout/keying, grounding, energy isolation, power sequencing/backfeed/inrush protection; safety circuit integration; authoritative lock/configuration sensors; approved numeric loads/tolerances/metrology; fault/partial-coupling disposition; local operator procedure, re-enable and authorized test/procurement decisions. If an independent upper requires missing stand/power/safety, or the base depends on removed upper support/control, that independent use case is blocked—not claimed by omission. A named supported configuration may be tested only at its own evidence ceiling.
7. **08C — physical ASSEMBLY-01; requires 08B, exact-component 06C+07C and separate coupling readiness.** Under the authorized stationary procedure, establish safe support/retention and isolate prescribed energy, couple, observe locks/power/safety, reconcile and explicitly re-enable. Demonstrate standalone upper LAB-01 and standalone base BASE-01 in their separately eligible configurations; reconnect must not move automatically.
8. Exercise only hazard-owner-authorized partial/failed-coupling and interruption cases, with retained dummy carriers and exclusion controls; verify independent safe disposition, withheld operation and explicit reconciliation/re-enable. Retain configuration/observer/metrology records for each mode and all failed/indeterminate attempts. Do not inject uncontrolled physical faults to satisfy software test coverage.
9. Track 08A/08B/08C separately; configuration changes require reassessment. Mark missing physical cases external-gated/unexercised, never shrink completion to only the assembled mode or label software composition as physical recovery.

## Security Considerations
Module identification is not authorization or safety eligibility. Bind private commands/observations to trusted local identities and the current configuration; use least-privilege capabilities and retain denied/stale outcomes. Preserve ADR0006/0007/0013 production/exact-device/independent-promotion gates. Product-scope approval is not blanket Law1 approval; cross-board transport or public interface changes require their own exact checkpoints, never remote-C2C or shared-memory workarounds.

## Risk Notes
Coupling changes load paths and energy hazards even when stationary; previous standalone acceptance cannot qualify the combined system. Disconnecting power or removing a module may itself remove holding force/support. Missing hardware/safety packages gate 08C without preventing useful 08A/08B work.

## Risk Assessment
Revert private assembly scenario/image changes and remove only run-owned scratch resources to reverse software changes. Reverting software cannot undo coupled mass, moved carriers, damaged locks, stored energy or a partial electrical connection. First use the exact independently safe physical procedure, retain/support and reconcile actual state, then inspect and explicitly re-enable; neither memory/VFS restore nor automatic reverse motion is physical rollback.
