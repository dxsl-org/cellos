## Adoption and evidence rules

- **Former owner** means the exact old plan entrypoint in each row, not an invented named maintainer. **Destination** means internal integration responsibility after P01 gates, not permission to execute.
- Read each of the 38 entrypoints' current metadata, lock notice and relevant phase/status/unfinished sections. Where relevant, read the subordinate ReadGrant-source, VFS caller-retirement, stateful-hotswap and Intel VMX gate sections named below. Historical records remain intact; their results are **recorded evidence**, not newly rerun or independently source/artifact-qualified results.
- **N — no unlock**, applied explicitly in every row: the former plan and its direct phase links remain blocked for the listed scope; no old cook/parallel/no-ABI instruction overrides the controller. Adopt unfinished prerequisites internally; never wait for a locked predecessor to complete or unlock. Prior unrelated blockers remain provenance or named external gates, not new backwards scheduling edges.
- **A — exact gate:** P01 census of changed public/persistent symbols, callers/tests/docs and owner; exact revision/digest plus Law-1 checkpoint 1 before edits; implementing evidence plus checkpoint 2 before freeze/promotion. Historical approval covers only its original bytes/semantics; architecture selection supplies neither checkpoint. This ledger is scoped-plan adoption, **not** the exhaustive affected-symbol/caller census.
- **L — local safety:** retain artifact/signing/policy intersection, launch edges, Tier-2 denial and object-specific issuer authority until an approved equivalent cutover. Prove incarnation, admission, retirement/revoke and cleanup on the exact supported profile; no fallback to ambient authority/SAS.
- **R — recovery:** independently prove lifecycle identity, authoritative committed watermark, acknowledged-state survival for the declared failure class, writer fence and unknown-outcome reconciliation. Warm-kernel snapshot is not a prerequisite for service recovery and remains separately closed.
- **P — protected:** preserve ADR-0008/0009, original protected relay **AC-001..AC-011 Build-entry** and **AC-012 post-Build activation** gates (these are not Spec 25 AC-01..AC-12). Relay-only proof precedes separately qualified direct LAN. Hardware/cloud/provisioning/production authorization is not inferred.
- **Q — qualification:** retain original ceiling; host/model, exact QEMU architecture, Intel VMX/nested lane, physical Intel, protected service and production are separate claims. P10 records exact source/artifact/configuration/logs/negative controls and qualifies all 50 ACs.
