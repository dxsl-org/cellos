## Selected proposal and representation boundary

PROPOSED immutable origin tuple: admitted source node/boot/Cell incarnation, operation/request identity, workload/service/method/schema, canonical typed payload **or authenticated digest**, original deadline and granted authority/effect/resource scope. A reusable subject-may-call assertion is insufficient. Transport peers cannot supply their own trusted CellId. Payload serialization/digest algorithm and proof bytes are exact I1/I2 inputs, not guessed from V1.

The origin kernel or assigned approved operation issuer owns a bounded immutable attempt record. Router proposes one eligible C06 Ready member/route revision; that **same origin issuer**, not router/controller/Authority, atomically seals exact concrete target, Ready/member/provider/export generation and approved epoch attenuation. At most one destination binding for the origin operation. Retrieval returns that identical binding; payload/deadline/request/target/route mutation refuses. No per-request global Authority/controller hop or signature work for ordinary local calls; local immutable records are checked directly.

PROPOSED new versioned origin/attenuation/correlation carrier and operation record. Existing V1 bytes/reserved discriminants remain untouched until approved clean cutover. Origin record identity/lifetime/exhaustion, canonical payload choice and authenticated channel/proof encoding are blocked, not selected arbitrarily.

## State transitions and ordering

1. `OperationReserved -> OriginCaptured`: P04 reserves operation/completion/output capacity before acceptance; capture attested source/authority and immutable canonical payload/deadline. No reusable origin proof can later mint a fresh operation identity.
2. `OriginCaptured -> Queued`: router holds bounded pre-dispatch queue under original deadline and subject/byte limits. Expiry/cancellation before selection produces no target submission and no late execution.
3. `OriginCaptured/Queued -> TargetSealed(T)`: after bounded C09 eligibility/share reservation, origin issuer performs compare-once seal. Same exact binding retrieval succeeds; a second binding or mutable origin field refuses even if first destination refused or never replied. Local unsubmitted share may be rolled back only as proven local bookkeeping, never to reseal another target for this operation.
4. `TargetSealed -> Submitted/DefiniteNotSubmitted`: dispatch/handoff records ADR-0009 acceptance boundary. Exactly one selected submit attempt; definite destination refusal is returned, not hidden by another target/hedge/fallback. Unknown accepted handoff becomes uncertain, not a retry budget.
5. Destination validates immutable origin and target attenuation, assigned Ready/live export/state fence, deadline scope and original subject before effect; atomically correlate/dedup exact origin identity and payload/target. Same proof lookup returns retained status/result or Busy/uncertain, never another effect. Two destinations cannot both obtain valid origin-issued targets.
6. `Submitted -> TerminalRetained/Unknown -> AuthoritativelyConsumedOrReconciled`: retention keeps origin attempt identity and terminal/uncertain correlation. Cache expiry or transport reconnect cannot transition same origin to Dispatch. A later genuinely independent caller request can be separately authorized; router cannot mint it from old proof.

## Errors, outputs, lifetime and side effects

PROPOSED refusal classes: wrong issuer/source incarnation/subject/scope, changed payload/schema/deadline/operation, wrong route/Ready/generation/target, second-target request, expiry or exhausted attempt storage. Before submission output remains unmodified and no effect proof is emitted on refusal; after handoff cancellation/disconnect/timeout/reply loss is uncertain when required. Response/reply/cancel correlation binds both incarnations plus exact operation/export/session; no free result through borrowed mutable input.

Exact public error/status/output layout and short-buffer/copy-fault transaction behavior are CP1 inputs. The operation owner retains payload/proof pins or immutable copied bytes for their required lifetime. Origin attempt and destination outcomes outlive route cache and remain charged until authoritative consumption/reconciliation and safe replay fencing. History expiration is uncertain/refusal, never reexecution. Preserve explicit captured reply by another live thread of the **same accepted provider Cell incarnation**; do not invent per-thread distrust or transfer unrestricted caller result ownership.

## Units, bounds and charge owners

Operation/attempt/correlation/result slots, canonical payload/proof bytes, retention and local compare-once work are finite C02/P04 charges. Origin kernel/approved issuer owns attempt; router owns queue/share; destination effect issuer owns correlation; broker owns authenticated transport buffers. P01/P04/P08-A supplies exact retention/key exhaustion and footprint; no cache TTL is promoted to authority validity or release rule.
