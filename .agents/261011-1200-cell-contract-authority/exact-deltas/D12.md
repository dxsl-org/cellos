V1 forbids streaming. `MAX_C2C_PAYLOAD=min(local_ingress::MAX_REQUEST_BODY, api::ipc::NET_TCP_INLINE_DATA_MAX -16 Noise tag -112 header)`, not a universal transport ceiling. Encoder validates and checks output capacity before zero-fill/copy; decode borrows input without allocation, rejects reserved bytes/version/class/length/identity. A decoded envelope is not authenticated until the protected transport/admission producer validates it.

### Existing lifetime/replay semantics requiring amendment

- [RequestDeadline](../../../cells/services/net-broker/src/c2c_deadline.rs#L21-L55) computes checked receiver `now_ms + relative_ms`; before-dispatch expiry is Timeout, after-dispatch expiry Indeterminate. It is not synchronized origin-cutoff evidence.
- [ReceiveGate](../../../cells/services/net-broker/src/c2c_receive.rs#L28-L65) refuses nonincreasing server epoch replacement and stale destination before dedup; replacing server retires response entries while preserving source replay floors.
- [DedupKey](../../../cells/services/net-broker/src/c2c_dedup/types.rs#L5-L15) is source node/boot/request + destination server epoch, lacking source Cell/operation/payload/schema/single-target authority. Existing16-entry cache/16 source windows and30,000 ms TTL are source constants, not new admitted profiles.
- [dedup begin](../../../cells/services/net-broker/src/c2c_dedup.rs#L31-L58) redispatches expired Completed + Idempotent; [test oracle](../../../cells/services/net-broker/src/c2c_dedup/tests.rs#L81-L97) expects it. This materially conflicts with the selected one-origin/one-target attempt contract and must migrate, not be relabeled compliant.

## Proposed semantics and carrier boundary — design-proposed

1. Keep concrete local/remote endpoints pinned to an accepted node/boot/Cell/export/provider generation. Do not retrofit balancing into old constructors. Raw-TID LocalEndpoint::new remains explicitly unattributed until its governed migration; resolving or serializing an endpoint never installs resource/method authority. Logical pool is a separate opt-in typed descriptor with replication/schema/affinity class governed by C01/C06, not an implicit fallback.
2. Keep current remote `NotSupported` refusal closed until real authenticated issuers, dispatch and protected gates exist. Do not implement transmission with synthetic fixture constructors or make validation-only new() a credential factory. No source preparation here changes the output untouched/no-contact guarantee.
3. Select a **distinct versioned canonical proof-bearing carrier** for target remote requests, rather than extending V1 reserved bytes, interpreting payload as an unreviewed authority trailer, or consuming existing kind/retry values. Exact version/name/layout remains blocked. V1 reservations and old method/config meanings stay occupied until clean release; rejection/removal of old remote artifacts must be explicit, not permanent aliases/shims.
4. Target request binds authenticated source node/boot/**Cell incarnation**, origin operation/request, application/workload, exact method/schema and canonical typed payload or authenticated digest, original deadline and authority/effect scope. Authorized origin issuer seals at most one concrete selected target/Ready member/route revision/provider epoch attenuation; retrieving again returns the same binding. Router cannot mint a second target/request/payload/deadline. C07 owns bytes and issuer lifetime; a bigger broker envelope cannot manufacture them.
5. Destination validates original subject, live method/export/resource scope, concrete generation, Ready/state fence and admission atomically before dispatch. C06 Ready comes from assigned destination supervisor/kernel/export/state authority, not controller-only membership or existing service/epoch seed. Broker sessions/Noise/node keys authenticate transport, not lifecycle/child budget or VFS/NET rights.
6. No retries/hedges/alternate targets after submit, including Busy/refusal/uncertainty. RetryClass remains a method annotation, not permission to redispatch an existing immutable origin operation. Replace expired-Idempotent effect redispatch with retained-result replay when authoritative and otherwise Indeterminate/closed rejection; neither expiry nor cache eviction authorizes a fresh effect. Exact retention/exhaustion/tombstone policy awaits C07/profile input; no unbounded history invented.
7. Separate **NotSubmitted** (definite no accepted handoff) from **Submitted** (accepted/ambiguous handoff); after dispatch/commit uncertainty cannot become definite Timeout or successful cancel. Preserve one terminal/one consuming result and same-incarnation captured reply-token handoff from D04. Transport reconnect may restore session but never replay effects.
8. Preserve original deadline and subtract queue residence; destination receipt-relative budget is distinct. Origin-cutoff-sensitive effects require qualified authenticated time/error bounds plus commit-time checks, otherwise refuse that claim. Receiver-start freshness rounds C08 validate route/health/capacity from authorized source sets; unseen higher sequence or drip arrival cannot renew expired evidence.
9. Remote data is copied serialized payload or approved issuer-authorized exported reference; never raw SAS address, local fd/FILE/GrantId/ring pointer/local live cap. Long-running streams need their own admitted bounded lifetime/accounting, not silent V1 streaming.

## Lifetime/error/output and negative obligations

| Scenario | Design-proposed oracle / current boundary to preserve | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Current RemoteEndpoint::call | Returns NotSupported, no encoding/broker contact/output mutation until separately governed activation. | P08-A/B |
| Unsupported version/kind/retry, nonzero reserved, exact-length mismatch, oversized/zero IDs | Reject before admission/dispatch; encoder validation/short-buffer failure does not touch output; borrowed decode cannot outlive source frame. | P08-A/P04 |
| Authenticated session but wrong Cell/method/app/artifact/tier/resource | Destination refuses; transport keys/discovery/config names do not grant scope or budget. | P08-A/C/P02/P05 |
| Forged controller Ready, stale boot/export/Cell/state fence | No live admission/route member; old pinned endpoint never transparently binds new provider. | P08-B/C/P06 |
| Copied origin proof with changed payload/deadline/target/schema/request | Origin issuer refuses second attenuation; destination validates unchanged operation and scope. Two replicas cannot both effect one operation. | P04/P08-B/C |
| Cache expiry/eviction/completed Idempotent duplicate | No new dispatch/effect; replay only authoritative retained result, otherwise uncertainty/refusal. Retention exhaustion refuses new admission rather than erases uncertainty. | P04/P08-A/E |
| Deadline/cancel/partition around handoff/dispatch/commit | Definite pre-submit refusal versus submitted uncertainty; after-dispatch expiry/cancel never proves no effect. Reconnect does not retry. | P04/P08-A/E |
| Provider/receiver restart and counter exhaustion | New incarnation cannot adopt stale operation/result/challenge; checked exhaustion denies, no wrap alias or higher-sequence TTL renewal. | P02/P08-A/B |
| Small result output, origin owner exits, late reply | D04 terminal retained/reserved on small output; one consuming take; owner-exit cleanup linearized without duplicate effect or foreign-generation reply. | P04/P03 |
| Raw local cap/grant/fd/ring pointer in remote request | Reject authority interpretation; only approved serialized data/export references allowed. | P05/P08-A |
| Saturation, unknown lifecycle publication, partitioned Authority | Refuse before effect at bounds; unknown charge retained; no timeout Gone, second ledger or offline publication. | P08-C/P06 |

Borrowed frame/payload lifetime ends before source buffer reuse; accepted operations must own bounded copied data or approved pinned ownership. Encoder failure writes nothing; successful result writes declared bytes only. New wire result/error tag integers are unselected; current RemoteCallError Rust variants are NoService/Unreachable/Timeout/Busy/Indeterminate/AuthFailed/ProtocolError/NotSupported, not allocated wire status codes.

## Units, bounds and charge owners

Old deadlines u32 milliseconds and broker receipt clock u64 milliseconds are baseline; no synchronized origin cutoff or CPU quota conversion. Header112/Noise16/frame formula, peer16/method8/inflight16/per-peer4/per-peer bytes2×frame, dedup16/source16/TTL30000 are source constants, **not** new cluster maxima. P08-A reserves broker session/frame/queue metadata; NET owns IP/TCP resource charge; P04 origin operation/completion/retained outcomes; P08-B queue/member/share/challenge tables; P08-C independent Authority charged control/replica/full-vector allowance. Nested shares narrow parent, never duplicate it. Accepted/unknown/draining/retained/quarantined state stays charged until authoritative release; discovery/dedup expiry cannot reclaim lifecycle allowance.
