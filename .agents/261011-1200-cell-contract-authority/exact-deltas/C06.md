## Selected proposal and representation boundary

PROPOSED: retain every existing concrete endpoint's pinned meaning. Introduce a **separate, explicitly requested typed logical-pool descriptor**, admitted only for C01-approved service/method/schema/effect and replication class. Existing constructors do not silently balance, fallback remotely or change provider generation. Stateless interchangeable methods may choose any eligible member; shard/writer/session methods require exact approved ownership-map revision and owner. Unavailable owner is unavailability, not another writer/session.

Ready semantic tuple: assigned destination issuer identity, application/workload, Authority reservation and create key, measured artifact/template, admitted tier, node/boot/Cell/export generation, service/method schema, restored/initialized status, admitted resources and live state-fence requirement. Controller can publish this evidence but **cannot attest it**. Router verifies assigned issuer/provenance and policy independently off the request hot path; destination rechecks live authorization/export/admission/state fence at dispatch. Ready is eligibility evidence, never cached authorization or offline budget.

PROPOSED distinct versioned Ready/member and logical endpoint carriers. Do not extend LiveDestinationProof by inference, claim ProtectedEpochProof is original subject proof, allocate reserved V1 bytes or rename old concrete endpoints. Exact public symbol spelling/layout/version/authenticator, issuer trust chain and schema fingerprint remain blocker-bound.

## State transitions and validation order

1. `Reserved -> Preparing`: C04 live-claimed keyed publication exists; initialize/restore service under local resources and P06 state authority. No route membership yet.
2. `Preparing -> ReadyEvidenceIssued`: assigned destination/kernel/export/state issuer confirms exact key/incarnation/artifact/tier/schema/export and state fence. Failed/unknown preparation or lost restore state cannot issue Ready.
3. `ControllerPublication -> RouterValidating`: bounded parse/version/canonical/duplicate checks; C08 challenge binding/controller epoch/revision; C01 application/schema/class/affinity; independently authenticate every member's Ready issuer and tuple, including matching reservation/create key and exact generation. Reject the new whole snapshot on invalid evidence, leaving prior snapshot only until its original expiry; no partial forged-member installation or TTL renewal.
4. `RouterValidating -> Eligible`: bounded C09 cycle/share preparation completes before atomic publication. Candidate still requires fresh route/health and correct affinity/share on selection; C07 seals one concrete target.
5. `Eligible -> Suppressed/Draining`: stale health/challenge, revoked export/writer fence or explicit drain removes new selection; destination cutoff independently refuses stale routes. Already admitted operations retain target/lifetime. Removing Ready never proves Gone or release.
6. Restart/generation replacement rejects predecessor evidence/shares unless C04/C09 reconcile/fence it. Reboot may prove a particular old child Gone through authoritative lifecycle evidence, but never authorize replacement or release without closure.

## Errors, outputs, lifetime and side effects

PROPOSED refusals: non-opt-in method, incompatible replication/effect/schema, wrong application/key/artifact/tier/generation/issuer, unavailable affinity owner, stale evidence, revoked/live admission failure. Concrete current RemoteEndpoint remains NotSupported while gates closed; proposed logical error/status codes are unallocated. Rejected descriptor/membership emits no usable endpoint and mutates no caller result/output, route, allowance or writer right. Exact output/copy-error carrier belongs to CP1.

Router retains validated bounded membership/cache until receiver-start expiry; operation-owned target binding and terminal correlation can outlive cache. Streams remain pinned active work; replacing Ready cannot migrate them. Destination Ready proof expiry is not operation terminality. Controller outage permits only still-valid routing plus live destination authority; no closed Authority mutation is replaced with a cached grant.

## Units, bounds and charge owners

Finite logical endpoint descriptors, members, proof bytes, schema/affinity entries and validation work per publication; preallocated C09 cycle/share/queue storage. Router/control envelopes and C02 application allowance own all cache/proof/storage charge. Destination resource/state issuers own Ready facts; controller owns publication policy; Authority alone owns allowance. Artifact digest/schema/generation/epoch widths and lifetime values are unselected. [Profile budgets](../profile-budgets.md#required-parameter-ledger) requires actual proof/table/storage costs.
