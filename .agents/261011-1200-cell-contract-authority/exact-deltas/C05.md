## Selected proposal and representation boundary

PROPOSED: freeze immutable incarnation authority/resource maxima `M` at creation, maintain independently mutable admitted allocation `A <= M`, and measure current use/obligations `U` through authoritative local owners. Only authorized budget issuer changes application ceiling; Authority enforces it; controller only orchestrates. Application pending target and effective charged ceiling are distinct, so below-use shrink never pretends charges already disappeared.

For growth target `A'`, reserve `delta_plus[d] = max(A'[d] - A[d], 0)` at Authority and destination **before use/publication**, even for mixed grow/shrink vectors; no optimistic netting against unreleased negative components. Above-M growth refuses that incarnation and needs a separately authorized budgeted replacement. Resize keys bind exact incarnation, old/new allocation revision/vector, Authority/controller fence and operation identity; repeat reconciles the same resize.

PROPOSED versioned current-allocation/resize-intent/pending-ceiling/drain-cutoff/retirement records, not quota-default mutation or rights-max rewrite. Exact representation and backend atomicity are blocker-bound below.

## Errors, outputs, lifetime and side effects

PROPOSED refusal classes: unauthorized issuer/current-revision mismatch, malformed vector/overflow, immutable-max violation, local/global exhaustion, stale fence and authority unavailable. Below-use shrink is explicit **pending**, not a successful reduction or an error suppressed as success. Unknown grow/drain returns reconciliation-required tied to same key/incarnation; expose no free budget or larger current-limit handle before confirmed enforcement. Exact ABI codes and copy-fault/short-output behavior remain CP1 inputs.

Resize intents, cutoff acknowledgements, old allocation revisions, admitted rights, terminal outcomes and writer-watermark proofs remain retained until authoritative settlement. Rollback after durable growth/drain is recovery, not binary state reversal. Closing destination admission does not discard pre-cutoff work; controller/Authority timeout, missing telemetry and transport loss cannot classify Gone or release uncertainty.
