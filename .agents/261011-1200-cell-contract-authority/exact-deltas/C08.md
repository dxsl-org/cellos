## Selected proposal and representation boundary

PROPOSED one bounded receiver-owned round per approved observation kind/policy/source-set version at a time. Round tuple: receiver incarnation, workload/application/policy revision, observation kind (route/health/capacity/telemetry), **exact expected source identities and generations**, unique nonce, receiver-local monotonic start `S`, positive lifetime `L`, checked expiry `E = S + L`. The source receives and authenticates the challenge, **then** generates its current publication/sample and binds nonce/round/source epoch/sequence/policy/content. Precomputed queued data cannot be relabeled with a fresh nonce.

Receiver accepts each expected source's authenticated record at most once while its round is active and local `now < E`. Validity begins at **S**, never arrival; later arrival leaves less time, not a full TTL. Epoch/sequence protects rollback/replay but even an authentic previously unseen higher sequence arriving at/after E rejects. Packet drip and restart cannot renew expiry. A new completed live round alone supplies renewed evidence.

PROPOSED separate versioned challenge/sample/source-set carriers; receiver S and expiry are locally trusted state, not source-clock assertions. Exact nonce construction/width, incarnation/sequence encoding, observation discriminants, canonical bytes/authenticator/time unit and lifetime/profile limits are blocker-bound. No wire opcode/reserved field is selected.

## State transitions and validation order

1. `Idle -> Open(round)`: reserve finite round/source/response storage, authenticate admitted expected-source set, assign nonreusable receiver-incarnation nonce, snapshot C01 policy/set version, read monotonic S and checked E before sending challenge. Clock error/overflow or exhausted nonce space refuses opening; no zero/unbounded lifetime.
2. Source `ChallengeReceived -> SampleProduced`: verify receiver authority/scope/round then collect current record; authenticate exact challenge and source incarnation/epoch/sequence/content. Cached prechallenge record is not a valid response.
3. `Open -> SourceAccepted`: bounded parse/version/canonical checks; require exact receiver/nonce/kind/policy/source-set and expected live source identity; verify assigned publisher and generated-after-challenge binding; check now < E and nonregressing epoch/sequence; accept only once for that source. Wrong/duplicate/stale responses do not mutate counters or expiry. They cannot be used to claim completeness.
4. `Open -> Complete`: all required sources exactly once under this one round; atomically publish validated snapshot with original S/E, not a merge of different rounds or source generations. Route/member validation additionally consumes C06; telemetry completeness additionally consumes C10.
5. `Open/Complete -> Expired` at now >= E: stop using snapshot for selection/placement/scaling; retained operations stay charged. Superseding round invalidates predecessor responses; an invalid new round cannot refresh prior snapshot. Prior valid data, if retained during refresh, keeps its own original E only.
6. Receiver restart creates a new attested incarnation and closes predecessor rounds. Source-set replacement requires owner-authorized version and generation fences; no reset to empty/zero sources. Nonce/sequence exhaustion closes admission rather than wrap/reuse.

## Errors, outputs, lifetime and side effects

PROPOSED rejection classes: malformed/unsupported, wrong receiver/round/nonce/kind/policy/source, untrusted publisher, duplicate response, source rollback, expired/superseded round, missing time or bounded table exhaustion. Rejection emits no valid sample/route/capacity and cannot renew a window, zero demand, release budget or open a new effect. Exact error/status/output bytes remain CP1 input; parser refusals leave caller output/installed snapshot unchanged.

Route/health expiry suppresses new selection, capacity expiry blocks positive feasibility, and telemetry incomplete/expired freezes **both** scale directions. None proves Gone, terminal work or transferable shares. Round response and replay/nonce state remains bounded and charged through approved retirement; predecessor operation/share obligations outlive round expiry. No source timestamp or clock synchronization is assumed; C11 qualified origin cutoff is a separate prerequisite.

## Units, bounds and charge owners

S/L/E use a single receiver-local monotonic time unit; checked addition and strict expiry boundary selected, exact unit/L unselected. Finite rounds, expected sources, response/sample/proof bytes, sequence/nonce retention and validation work are preallocated C02 router/controller envelopes. Receiving router/controller owns freshness state; approved source owns postchallenge record; Authority/state owners still own allowances/fences. P01/P08/P03 supplies measured source/table/cost profile; heartbeat gap 1500ms is not a selected challenge lifetime.
