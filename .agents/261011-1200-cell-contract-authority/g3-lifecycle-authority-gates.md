## 4. Proposed transition and charge contract

Let K identify one immutable create binding; T identify a journal transaction; V be the complete approved C02 vector. For each dimension, effective application charge includes each control/child reservation exactly once, including Preparing/Claimed/Live/Draining/Unknown, plus unreleased resize positive deltas. Nested control/share allocations narrow their parent envelope and are not a second allowance. Within an admitted ledger, aggregate charge must not exceed the effective admitted ceiling. If recovery cannot prove that invariant or its floor, mutation remains closed rather than reconstructing a smaller favorable balance.

### 4.2 Closing, delayed create and credit once

1. WHEN closure begins, Authority SHALL durably install **Closing(K)** under the claim/fence serializer before accepting another claim. A previously confirmed claim may already have an in-flight destination publication; Closing alone is not proof it did not happen.
2. Destination SHALL close publication for K under the **same local gate as keyed publish**, preserve/commit the nonregressing durable fence/tombstone and reconcile any publication already won by that key. Closing new request admission is a separate cutoff and cannot substitute for publication-key fencing.
3. Destination/resource/state owners SHALL drain retained work under original rights/deadlines, establish the required writer watermark/effect fence and quiescence, then prove exact absence/Gone. The acknowledgement binds K, immutable binding, original node/boot/incarnation, Authority fence, local closure revision/floor and owner facts. A never-claimed key still needs enforceable nonpublication, not an empty map lookup.
4. Only after durable enforced closure **and** authoritative absence/Gone **and** all required quiescence acknowledgements may Authority atomically commit Closed/Released(K) with **one** credit. A lost reply resolves by original-key lookup of that committed revision. Repeated close/release cannot refund twice.
5. Destination recovery SHALL load/prove closed-key nonregression before opening publication. A delayed/queued/replayed create rechecks current live Authority admission/fence and local key closure at the publication gate. Regression, absent required tombstone or unprovable floor seals admission. No successful post-release delayed publication is allowed.

The durable close protocol must specify every inter-owner crash cut; no distributed atomic transaction is presumed. In particular, local gate-closed but tombstone not durable is not a successful close acknowledgement, and Authority Closing with destination unreachable retains charge. Reaching local Retiring/Empty in S3/S4 does not close a remote creation key.

### 4.3 Resize, drain and replacement

- WHEN target A′ is proposed for incarnation maxima M, each component SHALL satisfy A′ ≤ M and valid units/revisions. Authority reserves `delta_plus[d] = max(A′[d] - A[d], 0)` in every dimension before additional use, including mixed grow/shrink targets; never net against unreleased shrink. Destination live-claims/reserves matching local enforcement first.
- WHILE any authoritative use/retained obligation exceeds its A′ component, the **entire vector** SHALL remain unapplied: positive reservations remain charged but unusable, negative components remain installed at A, and the target is explicitly ShrinkPending. Preserve granted rights/work; do not kill or revoke to fit. Close affected creates/growth.
- WHEN all target dimensions fit under the local admission/use serializer, destination SHALL atomically install the whole A′/revision and record its result. Only an acknowledged safe local fence/use result allows Authority to release proven-unused negative delta once. Unknown installation retains all positive delta and blocks conflicting resize; query the same resize key/revision.
- IF application target B′ is below charge, issuer-authorized PendingTarget is distinct from effective ceiling B. Close new creates/growth and drain safely until charge fits; only then atomically adopt B′. Controller cannot mint a new maximum.
- Drain orders route suppression, destination new-admission cutoff, key-publication fence, retained operation/resource/state settlement, quiescence/Gone, durable key closure and release. Original admitted operations/results/pins/DMA/leases remain owned. Partition/timeout remains pending/charged, not a writer clone or effect replay.
- Beyond-M growth requires a separately authorized budgeted replacement. Old Draining/Unknown and new reservation coexist as charges until old safe release. Resource/concurrency class change removes homogeneous C10 scaling membership pending explicit approved reclassification; no silent capacity/target reinterpretation.

## 5. Unknown ownership and journal evidence gate

Unknown is an epistemic status of the **original** transaction/key/charge, not another grant and not a free balance. Before the first possibly accepted external write/publication, the candidate must identify recoverable ownership of full prospective charge, journal slots, outcome/closure retention, recovery workspace and reconciliation work. A memory-only owner object or Drop refund is insufficient for the selected owner-exit/node-failure class.

For each one-owner transaction, the candidate must prove: reserve capacity/prospective charge → immutable complete payload → **D1** payload/namespace/allocation durability → immutable successor commit → **D2** commit durability → acknowledge. Balance and corresponding key/state/outcome changes are one transaction. After possible acceptance, short/failed writes, D1/D2 failure, lost transport/reply or output-copy failure retain Unknown(T); they do not mean NotSubmitted. Conflicting mutations remain closed until exact reconciliation. No usable grant or claim proof is emitted on uncertain acceptance.

Recovery must start from an **admitted initialized genesis/checkpoint/floor**, authenticate bindings/chain and distinguish orphan/uncommitted/invalid/foreign/missing state without manufacturing WRITABLE. A payload digest alone does not authenticate a ledger or its genesis. The S9 counterexamples show this gate is not presently satisfied. Checkpoint/compaction must preserve every Unknown/outcome/closed-key obligation and prior acknowledged root until durable switch and proven replay-safe retirement. Exhaustion refuses new admission; it never evicts unresolved history or adopts an arbitrary TTL. Exact finite layout and lifecycle-owner implementation/evidence remain required beyond the limited G2 experiment.
