## 5. D04 — Operations, captured reply handoff and completions

**Mandatory invariant:** retain an inbound `current()` token before nested/deferred receive, and allow explicit `reply(captured_token, ...)` by another live thread of the **same accepted provider Cell incarnation**. Threads share trust; do not add a per-thread distrust barrier. This reply handoff is distinct from the target's explicit **caller result-consumption handle** handoff: handing off reply authority does not transfer caller owner-thread lifetime, reservation or unrestricted result draining.
