## Trust and availability tradeoff

Budget Authority and admitted destination kernels are the budget enforcement TCB; controller is policy-only and cannot mint allowance. Compromised TCB is outside this guarantee. Independent Authority adds a management-path live-claim dependency: partition closes new spawn/resize/share mutation, but does not invent another budget home. Already admitted operations remain bounded and retain explicit outcomes. Authority restart recovers the same durable logical ledger and monotonic fence; no empty reinitialization.

Single-origin-attempt state is origin-local, bounded and operation-owned, not a new global request ledger. No automatic replay after origin loss is introduced. Fixed-home Authority failover, consensus, resharding, DSM and cross-node threads remain out of scope.
