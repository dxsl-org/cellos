# Durable charge proof acceptance — local semantic contract candidate

2026-10-15. **Unapproved documentation candidate; no implementation, CP1 consent, issuer assignment, backend/floor admission or runtime qualification.** This document selects local acceptance requirements, not external proof bytes or real operational authority. All existing phases, locks and checkpoint pairs retain their recorded status. Historical `exact-deltas/C03.md` and DCO1 r1 remain unchanged.

## 1. Governing inputs and decision boundary

Governing inputs are the [ownership/adoption contract](durable-charge-ownership-adoption-contract.md), [DCO1 r1 byte candidate](durable-charge-canonical-byte-candidate.md), [current-allocation semantic candidate](durable-charge-current-allocation-candidate.md), [assignment evidence register](durable-charge-assignment-evidence.md), [C03 release amendment candidate](c03-release-amendment-candidate.md), [G3 owner interfaces O1–O9](g3-lifecycle-authority-gates.md#3-required-owner-interfaces--proposed-obligations-not-existing-apis), and [issuer/persistence proof and trust contract](issuer-persistence-candidate.md#3-canonical-signingproof-contract--proposed-no-allocated-wire-bytes). The [formal review](durable-charge-formal-review.md) records the independent external and historical-package blockers.

Decision: accept a fact only from its assigned authoritative producer, for an exact canonical scope and operation, with committed state where required, a nonregressing recovery basis, a complete finite dependency set and enforcement at the actual mutation gate. Assemble facts; do not appoint a generic all-purpose signer. A supervisor/controller may relay evidence but does not thereby become its issuer. One principal may fill several roles only through separate explicit grants.

This closes the local *specification ambiguity* about mandatory proof semantics, owner coverage, negative evidence and dependency ordering. It does not assert those facts exist, that producers implement them, that bytes can already be parsed, or that any owner has accepted this choice. The assignment register reports actual issuer, successor, destination-closure, resource/state/effect, backend, floor, verifier and finite-profile assignments **UNAVAILABLE**. No test fixture, embedded CA, engineering owner label, local digest or development image can discharge those rows.

The words MUST/MUST NOT specify proposed acceptance requirements. Proof role names below are semantic roles, not wire-kind allocations, function symbols or new acceptance-ledger IDs. No signature/MAC suite, key ID, signature size, endpoint, device, principal or persistent floor value is selected here. No DCO1 kind number is allocated.

## 2. Canonical semantic binding and acceptance boundary

### 2.1 Common authenticated context

Every accepted proof MUST bind the following ordered semantic groups. Exact tags, widths, presence encodings and authenticated-byte boundaries require a separately reviewed carrier; omission cannot be treated as a default.

1. Protocol domain, carrier/schema identity and revision, proof role/purpose and lane; exact encoded body length and content binding; request/result discriminator.
2. Admission decision identity/revision, policy and issuer-roster identities/revisions; issuer principal and incarnation, assigned home and role; admitted public verifier/key-purpose and revocation/rollover revision; intended verifier/audience.
3. Cluster/application/workload and exact authoritative ledger/genesis; relevant destination/state/resource home and journal/volume identity. Cross-ledger bootstrap proofs explicitly name source and destination rather than conflating them.
4. Immutable debit origin `D`, obligation `O`, original reserve identity, `K_create`, applicable distinct resize/release key, adoption `A`, owner-local transition transaction `T_i`, side and immutable transition-map identity, according to role. Inapplicability MUST be explicit in the approved schema. `D` never changes when custody changes.
5. Exact canonical operation/method/schema, request length/digest and effect binding; full profile/dimensions/units and relevant charged/effective/prospective/pending vectors; immutable destination/artifact/template/tier/architecture and exact incarnation bindings.
6. Provenance owner versus current custodian, their separate epochs, current Authority enforcement fence and separate controller epoch, expected predecessor/revision, allocation revision where applicable, and bound dependency references.
7. Authoritative result/status and its exact scope, committed revision/digest and input/output floor roles where applicable; unresolved work, retained capacities, coverage roster/checkpoint identity and replay/retirement restrictions.
8. Exact request/response correlation: authenticated requesting and responding endpoint incarnations, operation/session/export where used, and the request identity. Fresh lookup admission uses the approved current gate/revision or challenge/session mechanism, never an invented wall-clock lifetime.

Canonical sets have a specified total order, exact counts, no duplicates, explicit coverage and checked finite limits. Vectors include every admitted dimension, including zeros, in the admitted unit/revision; roles cannot be exchanged because lengths match. Byte references bind exact schema/kind, encoded length and content digest, with all referenced bytes retained and independently verified. Unknown schema, unassigned producer, missing required field/referent, changed bytes under the same key, conflicting equal revision, cycle or count/work exhaustion MUST reject before usable permission or unbounded allocation. Neither a presentation name nor a caller-provided hash authenticates authority.

An accepted operation-specific internal fact MUST NOT become portable permission for another operation. Authentication and parsing may be cached only within their admitted policy/retention rules; mutable admission, revocation, custody and fence conditions are checked at the authoritative gate. A once-valid receipt cannot bypass a newer fence, Closing state or revoked grant.

### 2.2 Integrity, authentication, durability and permission are separate

| Layer | What it establishes | What it cannot establish alone |
|---|---|---|
| Canonical bytes/integrity | Exact content, schema/length and reference consistency | Producer identity, rights, completeness, commit, nonregression or truthful outcome |
| Authenticated authorized issuer | The assigned principal attested this exact purpose/scope/body under an admitted verification policy | Physical durability, absence of omitted owners, current admission or external quiescence |
| Qualified committed-result evidence | An exact owner-local transaction/result survives the admitted failure class | A cross-owner atomic transaction, resource outcome, or protection against a stronger rollback threat |
| Floor/current-chain and complete coverage | Admitted nonregression and complete relevant state/owner set | Mutation authority without the current custody/fence/claim gate |
| Atomic current gate | This exact action is admitted against current epoch/revision/closure | Permission to repeat an already uncertain effect or credit under a fresh identity |

DCO1's trailer is integrity only. A file read, successful readback, generic success/flush return or authenticated assertion from an unqualified storage adapter is not an admitted durable commit proof. No suite or signature is inferred from DCO1's content digest. Local authoritative checks may use an approved typed internal fact instead of a remote signed carrier; that path still needs exact producer/consumer ownership, scope, lifetime, serialization and qualification. This document does not require per-call signing or introduce a signing oracle.

## 3. Mandatory proof roles

### 3.1 Owner-local committed result

A commit proof MUST bind the common context, exact request and canonical committed record schema/length/digest, journal/ledger/genesis/home/volume, writer identity/epoch, original transaction and operation keys, checked expected predecessor revision/digest, resulting revision/digest, exact durable status, input and output floor roles and backend admission reference. It MUST attest only after the authoritative transaction and required barriers have committed under the admitted failure model. The consumer checks the record's payload/header/predecessor equalities, not just the carrier digest.

An authoritative same-transaction lookup can substitute for a lost commit reply only if it proves that exact committed result under the qualified current chain/floor. It MUST distinguish committed, terminal nonaccepted/aborted where defined, unresolved and recovery-failed. A transport error, failed output copy, short buffer, barrier error or missing response cannot prove nonacceptance. Unknown commits retain the original identity, charge and recovery capacity; retries query the same transaction, not reissue an effect or credit. Equal transaction with changed binding rejects.

### 3.2 Ledger, destination and owner nonregression floor

A floor proof MUST bind the assigned floor producer and its authority/admission revision; purpose/lane; exact ledger/genesis, destination or state/resource home; actual storage/volume identity; floor namespace/incarnation and revision; authenticated minimum accepted state/epoch/checkpoint identity or an explicit authenticated relation to it; predecessor/advancement relation; persistence and recovery policy; and the exact failure/threat class. Journal revision, allocation revision, boot identity, writer epoch and floor revision are distinct domains and MUST NOT be equated numerically.

A floor establishes a lower bound, not completeness or permission to select any later digest. Recovery requires an authenticated unique chain or admitted complete checkpoint at/above that bound, no conflicting branch, the current authorized custody/fence chain and all retained obligations. Merely choosing the largest observed counter or a higher unsigned/digest-valid record rejects. A stale but once-authentic floor is not proof of the current lower bound. The approved producer/consumer protocol MUST specify current-floor retrieval/pinning, concurrent advancement and crash handling; inability to establish the current basis keeps mutation closed.

The actual floor producer must classify protection honestly: a trusted nonregressing ordinary volume is not independent protected anti-rollback. Wrong volume, initialized-state disappearance, regressed state, incomparable floor, fork, unknown floor authenticity, exhausted epoch/revision or missing recovery dependencies keep the affected scope RecoveryFailed/mutation-closed. No reset, new genesis, formatting, lower-floor restoration or automatic home relocation is allowed. Floor reset/replacement requires a separately authorized forward-recovery/namespace-retirement decision with retained obligations; it cannot silently reactivate old work.

### 3.3 Enforced writer/admission fence

A fence proof MUST name the authorized installer and succession decision; adoption/operation scope; exact old and successor custodians/epochs; gate owner/home/incarnation and affected keys/methods/resources; durable non-abortable intent where required; installed fence revision/floor; last admitted and committed watermarks; and the complete unresolved old-work set or a bounded complete checkpoint covering it. Each relevant journal, publication, resource, state and effect gate provides its own acknowledgement. An aggregate manifest authenticates these acknowledgements; it cannot issue them on their owners' behalf.

Each gate MUST serialize fence installation with epoch validation plus mutation admission/commit. Validation before a pause followed by an unvalidated post-fence commit is forbidden. The acknowledgement MUST state what already admitted work may finish, what cannot be admitted/committed, and how retained old operations will be queried at original identities. It cannot assert blanket quiescence merely because a new epoch was installed. Gate restart recovers its admitted closed/fenced state before reopening admission; incarnation change without continuity proof is not a fence.

Missing, partial, uncertain or non-enforceable acknowledgement closes the relevant scope; it does not elect a successor or allow conflicting successor effects. Preauthorized recovery may supersede an unreachable old process without its signature, but only the assigned recovery/fence authority can make that decision. Timeout, PID/TID, restart counter, controller election and supervisor kill are not that authority.

### 3.4 Complete state and owner coverage

A completeness proof MUST bind the authoritative initialized scope, snapshot/checkpoint revision/digest/floor, admission cutoff/watermark, exact finite object and owner-roster identity/revision, exact entry counts, coverage/range rules and canonical manifest content. It MUST cover every charged obligation and every retained Unknown, prepared/assigned/accepted-but-unsettled adoption, debit origin, current allocation, pending resize/credit, outcome, closed key, duplicate-credit guard, capacity reservation, pin/lease/DMA, state/effect fence and replay/retirement dependency required by that scope. Singleton transfers still require completeness.

Completeness is proved by an authoritative enumeration/index/checkpoint protocol with admitted bounds, not directory walking, a truncated response, an entry count supplied by the requester, or a hash root with no coverage semantics. Paged evidence requires exact scope/snapshot identity, nonoverlapping coverage and a terminal complete marker; omissions, duplicate pages, changing snapshots and unknown remainder reject. An admitted checkpoint may summarize history only if it retains equivalent authoritative decisions, outcomes and replay protection and proves safe predecessor retirement.

The finite reconciliation roster is derived from admitted workload/method/resource policy **and** the complete durable grant/dispatch/delegation/dependency history, then bound into the closure or adoption snapshot. Each member names principal/incarnation/home, purpose, affected O/key/resource/effect and required acknowledgement roles. Every possible issuer of admitted work is represented, including exported grants, service-owned operations and external effect/state homes. Shared ownership is explicit; cardinality is not inferred from the number of responses.

During roster sealing, new admission/delegation that can add a dependency is closed or serialized into the authoritative history before the cutoff. An already admitted operation capable of creating a downstream dependency must prove its complete downstream set or remain unresolved; it cannot disappear because a roster was frozen. Discovery of an unlisted dependency invalidates completeness for mutation/release: retain charge and reconcile under a newly reviewed/sealed manifest where the transition permits, otherwise remain closed on the existing non-abortable transfer branch. No owner may designate itself irrelevant by silence.

### 3.5 Destination binding and authoritative publication status

Destination binding MUST cover application/workload/ledger/genesis, D/O/original reserve and create/operation keys, exact immutable request/effect digest and profile/vector roles, admitted destination node/boot/kernel/artifact/template/tier/architecture, target Cell/root/generation/export incarnation when published, Authority home/current fence, claim identity/revision and exact destination lifecycle intent/result/closed-key journal. Before publication, permitted absent result fields are explicit; after publication the original exact result is immutable and cannot be replaced by a new reusable task ID.

New publication requires the live Authority claim serialized against Closing/fence changes and independently checked local resource/policy admission. A prepared adoption receipt, offline reservation receipt or destination binding alone is not a live claim. Destination publication and durable closed-key fencing share the authoritative serialization required to reject delayed creates. Same-boot keyed map lookup returns at most the original incarnation; supervisor restart does not clear it. Destination intent precedes possible publication; uncertain publication is reconciled against the same key/map and remains charged.

Boot-Gone evidence MUST come from the assigned node/boot lifecycle producer, identify the old admitted boot and exact key/incarnation, prove that old boot cannot publish/execute again under the admitted model, and bind recovered closed-key/admission protection for later boots or replay-safe namespace retirement. A new boot observation alone supplies none of those facts. Gone for the kernel does not prove quiescence at remote resource/state/effect owners.

### 3.6 Closure, quiescence and negative evidence

A complete closure manifest MUST bind exact D/O/create key/request/destination/original incarnation, current allocation and charge state, current custody/writer, stable close/release identities, complete sealed owner roster, claim/admission cutoff, irrevocable destination key closure, authoritative absence/Gone, every required resource/state/effect outcome and quiescence acknowledgement, current floors and any retained replay protection. It is a collection of authoritative facts, not itself a refund or credit permission from a generic assembler.

Authoritative negative evidence MUST state an exact initialized covered scope and snapshot/cutoff revision, exact queried key/transaction/incarnation/binding, authentic current floor, complete index/history coverage and explicit terminal status. For safe nonacceptance it also proves every gate capable of later accepting that operation is durably closed or covered by a retained replay-safe fence. No missing file, NotFound from an incomplete map, generic refusal, timeout, partition, uninitialized journal, corruption, unobserved response or empty task list qualifies. An authentic Absence result without durable key closure does not authorize release because delayed work may still publish.

Every required roster member supplies either its positive terminal outcome/quiescence proof or explicit authoritative negative/no-obligation evidence at the sealed cutoff with no future late acceptance. An owner marked not-applicable needs a policy/dependency-coverage justification from the authoritative coverage producer; an empty field or unsigned caller assertion is not negative evidence. A live owner with retained results, grants, pins, DMA, leases or state/effect Unknown is not quiescent merely because no current task runs. Ambiguous external outcome remains Unknown and charged, even if local publication is Gone.

## 5. Ordering, cycle avoidance and late old work

### 5.1 Acyclic evidence construction

The semantic dependency graph MUST be finite and acyclic. The approved byte candidate must publish its typed edge roles and checked traversal bounds. A reference cannot identify its own containing record, depend on a mutable hash-only object, or indirectly include its own output digest.

1. Start from externally admitted issuer/role/backend/floor/genesis decisions and preexisting charged control capacity. Admission trust cannot be bootstrapped from a key contained only in the proof being verified. The initial control precommit roots Authority publication; Authority does not issue its own prerequisite live reserve.
2. Obtain authentic already committed input snapshots/floors; construct complete vector/allocation/dependency/owner manifests and the immutable A/transaction map from retained facts. Capacity reservation precedes records depending on its capacity manifest. Input watermarks name already committed state, not the record being encoded.
3. Commit the owner-local transition by checked predecessor/revision serialization. DCO1 r1's top-level Watermark remains its immediate input predecessor; nested source preparation watermarks retain their different role. No semantic reinterpretation of r1 is authorized here.
4. Produce an authenticated committed-result carrier referencing that exact committed record and output revision/digest. Where floor advancement is needed for usable output, the separate floor protocol pins the input basis, advances/binds the admitted output state and supplies its result before activation. The record does not embed its own resulting floor carrier. A floor carrier may reference the committed record and prior floor, but not a commit carrier that in turn requires that same floor as a hashed child. Use separately ordered carriers or a separately reviewed later result schema, not a hash cycle.
5. A crash/uncertainty between record commit and required floor/result publication leaves mutation closed. Recover the exact original transaction and authoritative floor relation; do not roll back to make a reply convenient or assume an unproven higher floor. If the external floor adapter cannot implement this ordering, that action is unavailable.
6. Bootstrap order is B1 → B2 → source FenceStarted commit → all enforced fence acknowledgements → B3 → B4 → B5. Aborted is the mutually exclusive source branch before FenceStarted, then destination tombstone → prepared-capacity reclaim. Closure precedes release; the release record does not occur in its prerequisite closure proof. The later committed release result binds both release and closure.

No cross-owner atomic commit is presumed. Every edge is an independently authenticated durable fact with explicit uncertain status. A source need not reference future B4 to commit B3; B4 references B3, then B5 references B4. A manifest binds historical state/retained obligations, not every future result that may later resolve them.

### 5.2 Frozen manifests and late work

A gate fence does not erase work admitted before its cutoff. Its acknowledgement carries a complete finite set/checkpoint of committed and possibly admitted old operations, original keys/transactions and reconciliation authority. Work not proven terminal remains charged and prevents conflicting successor effects. Where the owner's fence model permits pre-fence admitted work to finish, any late result is accepted only as evidence for that exact original operation under the retained chain, not as new old-epoch authority. The gate must reject any stale commit forbidden by its installed-fence contract.

Late facts are retained append-only and correlated to frozen obligations. They cannot silently replace the A/manifest/target/transaction-map identity or alter its charge at handoff. Before FenceStarted, a manifest-changing resolution requires a new reviewed preparation with exact branch status. Once FenceStarted may have committed, recover the same A/target forward with the frozen conservative manifest and retained unresolved dependencies; the authorized accepted successor reconciles late outcomes under original keys after acceptance. If required evidence is unavailable, stay closed, not abort or retarget. The transferred historical Unknown may remain a charged conservative mirror until the successor's separately committed current-state resolution.

Late old release/shrink results require authentic lookup of the original idempotent credit transaction and complete current-ledger reconciliation before any new credit; neither source nor successor may credit again because an acknowledgement arrived late. A late create after durable closure rejects at the exact destination gate across restart. Retained outcome/closed-key/fence protection cannot be retired while such old messages or delegated work remain possible under the admitted namespace model.

For the allocation-byte successor, late-work permission excludes source allocation-ledger mutation: all admitted allocation/credit/final-release transactions must be authoritatively resolved before source preparation selects its manifest. Preparation freezes those selected indices; FenceStarted atomically revalidates them and durably prevents all further old-custodian allocation/result commits at the same serializer before fence I/O. An uncertain admitted allocation transaction blocks this boundary. Only external destination/resource/state/effect outcomes may arrive late as retained evidence for accepted-successor reconciliation. They cannot change a frozen non-Closed entry into a terminal source snapshot or bypass its mapped materialization. These stricter rules are mandatory for PA-10 in the successor; a generic effect-gate acknowledgement permitting late work cannot substitute.
